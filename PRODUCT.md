# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + CSS + inline SVG, rendered to an A4 PDF with headless Chromium (Playwright). Source lives in `wakflow-catalogue/`. Chosen by Claude (delegated): the user asked for a PDF, and HTML-to-PDF gives full control of type, gradients and vector graphics.

## Users

Owners and managers of Indian small and medium businesses (travel, real estate, clinics, coaching, retail, D2C, services). They receive the catalogue on WhatsApp or email, mostly read it on a phone, sometimes print it. They are not technical. Their job: understand what Wakflow sells, find the product that fixes their slowest task, and book a demo.

## Product Purpose

Wakflow (wakflow.com) is a solo AI and automation agency run by Naman. It sells 17 connected products — WhatsApp, team inbox, CRM, AI agents (chat and voice), automation, content engines, outbound sales tools, websites and care — as done-for-you services. The catalogue must make the whole suite easy to scan and each product easy to want.

## Positioning

Wakflow runs its own travel brands, Ghoomo Saste Me (GSM) and Tripwaley, on the same products it sells. Every proof number comes from those brands, measured and dated (27 Sep 2026). Products work alone or together as one system: every message answered, every lead saved, every follow-up sent.

## Operating Context

- Source content: the Wakflow Catalogue folder (one Markdown file per product). Only **Part A** (website copy) may be used in customer material.
- The catalogue is shared as a PDF, A4 portrait, one page per product.
- Contact: wakflow.com and +91 96253 30270 (WhatsApp / phone).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 17 products (01–17) plus the platform overview (00). Official names must be used exactly (e.g. "Wakflow AI Voice Calling Agent").
- Flagship: Wakflow Personal AI Assistant (16).
- Never name underlying open-source software, servers or internal tools in customer material. Never call it proprietary.
- Client businesses stay anonymous (e.g. "a furniture retailer"); GSM and Tripwaley may be named.
- No invented numbers, testimonials, customer counts or ROI claims. Use only Part A proof figures.
- No prices are published; pricing is "setup fee + monthly plan", quoted after a call.

## Brand Commitments

Pinned by the owner for the website ("we keep this exactly"). For the PDF catalogue the owner asked
(27 Sep 2026) for a light edition: white with a shade of purple, less content, no boxes. The website's
black background does not apply to the catalogue; the colours below are used as solid colours on light.

- Website: black with neon accents. Backgrounds #000 / #0a0a0a / #111.
- Cyan #00C8FF main brand colour; Blue #4A90E2; Purple #7B35C1.
- Orange #FF8A00 → #FFB347 is the action colour (buttons, stars, badges).
- Green #10B981 / #34D399 for ticks and done states. Red #F87171 for "before"/problem states.
- Grey #8892A4 for body copy.
- Signature: cyan → blue → purple gradient on headline text, paired with orange gradient buttons.
- Fonts: Oxanium 600–800 (headings, numbers, buttons), DM Sans 400–600 (body), JetBrains Mono (small uppercase labels with wide tracking, like `// 01 THE TRANSFORMATION`).
- Logo: the owner's "WF" mark (`wakflow-catalogue/assets/logo-original.webp`), set beside the word WAKFLOW in Oxanium.

## Evidence on Hand

Measured proof from GSM and Tripwaley in each product's Part A "Proof" section (as of 27 Sep 2026), e.g. 91,907 WhatsApp messages, 20,407 inbox conversations, 11,476 GSM CRM customers (only 63 typed by hand), 68 pilot AI calls with 85% answered and 0.91 s first word, 51 of 53 assistant runs completed, Tripwaley site PageSpeed 92 mobile / 100 SEO. No client testimonials exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. One system, not a pile of apps — show how products connect.
2. Prove with our own brands' real, dated numbers.
3. Plain English for non-technical owners; Hinglish-friendly.
4. Results, not software — Wakflow builds and runs it for you.
5. Start with one product, add the rest later.
