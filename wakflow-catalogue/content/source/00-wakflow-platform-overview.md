### The problem
- **Your tools don't talk to each other.** A lead comes in on WhatsApp, gets typed into a spreadsheet by hand, and the follow-up depends on someone remembering to send it.
- **Enquiries get answered late, or not at all.** Nobody can watch WhatsApp, Instagram, a website form and a phone line at the same time — so some leads simply go cold.
- **Every extra app is another login, another bill and another thing to keep configured.** Small teams end up managing software instead of running their business.
- **When something breaks — a disconnected number, a stuck automation — nobody notices until a customer complains.**

### Key benefits
- **Every conversation lands in one place.** WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, website chat and email reach one shared inbox, so nothing depends on which phone someone happens to be holding.
- **An AI answers first, a person takes over when it matters.** Routine questions get an instant, accurate reply from your own prices and policies — in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Anything that needs judgement goes to your team without the customer repeating themselves.
- **Every lead lands in your CRM by itself, with its source attached.** No enquiry is typed in twice, and a follow-up task is created for your team automatically.
- **Follow-ups, reminders and reports run without anyone pressing send.** Payment reminders, booking confirmations and your daily summary happen on their own.
- **Your own AI assistant watches the whole business for you.** It checks every system every morning, tells you what needs attention, and does routine work on a schedule while you sleep.
- **Start with one product, add the rest later.** Every product works stand-alone or combined — nothing has to be rebuilt when you grow.
- **One team builds it and keeps it running.** You never manage the underlying software — you get the results, the reports, nightly backups and a team that answers when something needs fixing.

### FAQs
**Do I need to buy all the products?**
No. Most businesses start with one or two — usually WhatsApp Connect with the AI WhatsApp Agent or the Inbox — and add more as they grow. Everything connects later without starting over.

**Which product should I start with?**
Tell us the one task that costs your team the most time today. We recommend the product that removes that pain fastest — and show you how it connects to the rest when you're ready.

**How is it priced?**
A one-time setup fee, then a simple monthly plan — per WhatsApp number, per inbox agent, per CRM user or per automation pack, depending on the product. AI usage comes with a fair monthly allowance. We share an exact quote after a short call about your business.

**How fast can we go live?**
An existing WhatsApp number can be connected the same day. An AI agent trained on your prices and policies, or a full connected system, is scoped with you and delivered step by step, so you see results from the first week.

**Will my WhatsApp number get banned?**
We protect your number the way we protect our own: human-paced sending, messages only to people who contacted you or agreed to hear from you, and Meta's official WhatsApp API for bulk campaigns.

**Does the AI speak Hindi and Hinglish?**
Yes. Our AI agents answer in English, Hindi and natural Hinglish — the way your customers actually write and talk.

**Can my team use it on their phones?**
Yes. The team inbox has a mobile app, and your Personal AI Assistant lives in WhatsApp or Telegram.

**Do I need technical people on my side?**
No. You get the results — messages answered, leads saved, reports delivered. Our team builds, connects and maintains everything.

**Is my data safe?**
Your conversations and customer records are stored on private infrastructure we manage, backed up every night to separate storage and health-checked every morning (see **[Wakflow Care](15-wakflow-care.md)**). Where a client needs it, AI can run privately so data never leaves the server.

**What is the flagship product?**
**[Wakflow Personal AI Assistant](16-personal-ai-assistant.md)** — your own AI assistant that knows your business, watches your whole Wakflow setup, works while you sleep and asks before anything risky. It is the product that ties the rest of the platform together for a business owner.
