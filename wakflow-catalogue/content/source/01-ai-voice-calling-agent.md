### The problem

- **"By the time we call back, the lead has gone cold."** Enquiries arrive from Instagram, the
  website and WhatsApp all day. The team calls them the next morning, when the customer has already
  spoken to three competitors.
- **"We have more leads than people to call them."** Old enquiry lists sit untouched. Nobody has the
  hours to ring hundreds of numbers, and the good leads are buried with the dead ones.
- **"I never know what actually happened on the call."** Did the salesperson send the details? Did
  they fix a callback time? Were they rude? Without a recording and a written summary, every lost
  lead is an argument, not a lesson.

### Key benefits

- **Every lead called in about a minute.** A fresh enquiry is dialled about sixty seconds after it
  arrives — while the person is still thinking about you — at any hour your calling rules allow.
- **Sounds like a person, not a robot.** The first word comes in under a second after pickup, she
  can be interrupted mid-sentence, she hears tone as well as words, and she never repeats the same
  line twice.
- **Answers from your real data.** Prices, packages, policies, timings and stock come from your own
  catalogue, which you edit in a browser. She does not make things up.
- **Acts during the call.** She sends details on WhatsApp from your business number, books a
  callback at the time the customer says, records what they want, and flags a hot lead to your
  senior person — all while still talking.
- **Every call written down and scored.** Recording, transcript, AI summary, structured lead details
  and a quality score out of 100 with the reasons in plain language.
- **You change the script yourself.** Edit what she says in a browser and the very next call uses it.
  No developer, no waiting.

### Features

#### Natural conversation

- **Answers in under a second.** The greeting is prepared while the phone is still ringing, so the
  customer hears a voice the moment they pick up — not the silence that makes people hang up.
- **Hears tone, not just words.** Powered by Google's Gemini AI voice, she listens to the actual
  voice and replies with a voice. She can tell an interested "haan!" from a tired "haan..." and
  respond to each differently.
- **You can interrupt her.** If the customer starts speaking, she stops — mid-word, like a person.
  A small "hmm" or "achha" does not stop her; it tells her to carry on.
- **Small sounds while she thinks.** Short "hmm" and "mm-hmm" sounds, recorded in her own voice,
  fill the gap while she composes a reply, so the line never feels dead.
- **"Ek second, main dekh ke batati hoon."** When she looks something up, she says a short holding
  line in her own voice and gives the answer only when that line has finished.
- **Patient when the customer goes quiet.** Someone thinking about a price gets time. She waits
  before gently checking they are still there, and says a polite goodbye only after several
  unanswered checks.
- **Never the same sentence twice.** Check-ins, goodbyes and apologies are picked from a set of
  alternatives, so she never sounds like a recording.
- **Uses the customer's name like a person does.** About one reply in five — not in every sentence,
  and not never.
- **Backs off when the customer is annoyed.** She stops selling for a turn, apologises plainly and
  asks nothing.
- **Knows the time of day.** She apologises for calling late in the evening, and she never hangs up
  while the customer is still speaking.
- **Natural Hinglish, with Hindi and English.** Built for Indian customers who mix languages in the
  same sentence. Further languages are added per project.

#### Calling and campaigns

- **Instant call on every new lead.** Any form, chat or ad lead can trigger a call automatically,
  after a short delay you choose.
- **Bulk campaigns from a spreadsheet.** Upload a list of name, phone and a note, and the campaign
  works through it with its own script, hours and rules. The note tells her something about that
  specific person.
- **Calling hours you control.** Set the start time, end time and allowed days per campaign. A retry
  that falls outside the hours waits for the next opening — nobody is rung at 6 am.
- **Smart retries.** Busy, no answer, voicemail and network failures are each retried after a
  sensible gap. Invalid or rejected numbers are never retried.
- **Limits that protect your budget.** Maximum calls at once, maximum attempts per person, a ring
  timeout and a maximum call length, so nothing runs up a bill overnight.
- **Voicemail handling.** A separate short script for when the call reaches voicemail.
- **Clear call status for every call.** Fourteen separate states — from queued and ringing to busy,
  voicemail and invalid number — so you always know exactly what happened.
- **Carrier reasons shown in your panel.** When a phone network refuses a call, the reason appears
  on the call record instead of needing a support ticket.
- **Inbound answering.** The same agent, script and panel can answer calls to your own business
  line, so callers get a helpful voice instead of a ringing phone.

#### Actions during the call

- **Sends WhatsApp while talking.** "Main aapko WhatsApp par bhej deti hoon" — and the message
  really goes, in that moment, from your own business number.
- **Checks WhatsApp before offering.** While the phone is ringing, she checks whether the number can
  receive WhatsApp. If not, she gives the details on the call instead of promising a message that
  can never arrive.
- **Looks up full details on demand.** She knows a summary of your catalogue and fetches the full
  record only when asked, so the call stays quick.
- **Saves the qualified lead as she learns it.** Need, dates, group size, budget and notes are saved
  the moment she hears them, so a dropped call still leaves your team everything learned so far.
- **Books callbacks in the customer's own words.** She books only a day and time the customer
  actually says, and reads it back to confirm.
- **Flags hot leads without ending the call.** A big order or urgent buyer is flagged to a human
  immediately — and she keeps going, answering questions and fixing a callback.
- **Live transfer to your team.** A hot caller can be handed to a human salesperson on the same call.
- **Records a soft "no", a wrong number or an opt-out.** Every outcome is recorded with the reason.
- **Custom actions for your business.** Anything with an API can become something she does on the
  call — send a payment link, check an order, raise a ticket, book a calendar slot, check stock.

#### Quality and records

- **A quality score on every call.** Scored out of 100 by clear rules, with the reasons written in
  plain language — "asked the same question twice", "no callback time agreed", "did 82% of the
  talking".
- **Eighteen things measured on every call.** Repeated questions, promises without action, talk
  share, interruptions, name use, whether the lead was saved and whether a next step was fixed.
- **Scores re-run over past calls.** When a quality rule improves, every past call can be re-scored.
- **Stereo recordings.** Customer and agent on separate channels, so you can hear exactly who said
  what and when.
- **Full transcript and AI summary.** Every turn with timestamps, plus a summary, a lead score and
  the qualification fields.
- **Recordings kept safely.** Thirty days ready to play in the panel, then archived to your Google
  Drive with no time limit.

#### Compliance and safety

- **Opt-out that nobody can switch off.** "Don't call me again" puts the number on your do-not-call
  list, and every future call to it is refused automatically.
- **Honest about being an AI.** If a customer sincerely asks whether they are talking to a real
  person, she tells the truth.
- **Per-campaign compliance switches.** Calling hours, do-not-call check, AI disclosure, recording
  notice and a frequency cap — each campaign chooses its own.
- **Customer-requested calls handled correctly.** Someone who asked to be called can be called
  outside marketing hours; opt-outs are still always honoured.
- **Built for Indian TRAI/DLT rules.** Calling-hour enforcement, do-not-call checking and consent
  tracking per campaign.
- **Your data protected.** Encrypted secrets, two-factor admin login, signed data hand-offs, and
  recordings that are never exposed to the browser directly.

### How it works

1. **We set up your agent.** Your script, voice, catalogue, policies, calling hours and caller
   number, configured in the panel.
2. **A lead arrives.** From a form, an Instagram DM, a WhatsApp chat, an ad or a spreadsheet — and a
   call is queued.
3. **She calls within about a minute.** A natural Hinglish conversation that answers questions from
   your real data.
4. **She acts on the call.** WhatsApp details, a callback booked, a hot lead flagged, an opt-out
   recorded.
5. **Your team picks it up.** A task in your CRM with the recording, the summary and the lead
   details attached.
6. **We tune it every week.** The quality scorecard shows what to improve, and the script changes
   the same day.

### FAQs

**How is it priced?**
A one-time setup for your script, catalogue and voice, a monthly fee for the panel, hosting and
support, and call usage by the minute. An optional monthly tuning plan keeps improving the calls
using the quality scorecard.

**How long does setup take?**
Days, not months. Your script, catalogue, policies, voice and calling hours are all configuration.
Custom actions for your own systems take a little longer.

**Does it speak Hindi?**
Yes. It speaks natural Hinglish — the Hindi-English mix your customers actually use — and handles
Hindi and English sentences too.

**Will customers know it is an AI?**
It sounds natural, but if a customer sincerely asks whether they are talking to a real person, it
tells the truth. We never make it lie about that.

**What if a customer asks something it does not know?**
It answers from your own catalogue and policies, and does not make things up. If it cannot answer,
it says your team will follow up and books a callback.

**Can I change what it says?**
Yes. The script is edited in a browser, block by block, and the next call uses your change. Every
version is saved, so you can go back in one step.

**Will it call people who asked not to be called?**
Never. "Don't call me again" puts the number on your do-not-call list and every future call to it is
refused automatically — nobody can switch that off.

**Which number does it call from?**
From a business phone number you own. Details on WhatsApp go from your own business WhatsApp number.

**Can I check calls on my phone?**
Yes. The panel works on a mobile, including the recordings, transcripts and scores.

**Is my customer data safe?**
Secrets are encrypted, admin login supports two-factor, every admin action is logged, recordings are
private, and your call database is backed up every night.

**Does it work with my CRM?**
It records every call and callback task in the Wakflow CRM out of the box. Another CRM is connected
by mapping its fields.

**Is it legal to make AI calls in India?**
Yes, when it is done right. Calls to people who asked to be contacted are the use we recommend.
Calling hours, do-not-call checks and consent tracking are built in, and we guide you through TRAI/DLT
registration if you plan marketing campaigns to wider lists.
