### The problem

1. **"Our WhatsApp is on one phone."** If the person holding it is on leave, busy or asleep, customers
   wait. If the phone is lost or reset, years of conversations go with it.
2. **"We pay for ads, people message us, and half of them never get a proper reply."** Enquiries arrive
   at night, on Sundays and in bursts, and by the time someone answers, the customer has booked elsewhere.
3. **"Every enquiry has to be copied by hand."** Names and numbers get typed into Excel or the CRM one by
   one — when someone remembers — so follow-ups slip and nobody knows how many leads really came in.

### FAQs

**How is WhatsApp Connect priced?**
A monthly fee per connected WhatsApp number, plus a one-time setup fee. On the official WhatsApp Business
API, Meta's own template-message charges are passed through at actual cost. Replies inside a customer-started
chat stay free on Meta's side.

**How long does setup take?**
On your existing number, the same day — linking the number itself takes about two minutes. The official API
route depends on Meta's business verification, which we handle for you.

**Will my number get banned?**
WhatsApp acts against numbers that spam — messaging strangers in bulk, or getting blocked and reported a lot.
We build in human-like pacing, group filters and consent-first rules, and we move high-volume promotional
sending to the official API, which is designed for volume. Replying to people who contacted you first, at a
human pace, is exactly what WhatsApp is built for.

**Do I need a new number?**
No. Keep the number your customers already have saved. A new number is needed only if you want a separate
official-API line.

**Will WhatsApp still work on my phone?**
Yes. WhatsApp Connect works as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. You and your staff can keep chatting from
the phone, and the bot steps aside when a person replies.

**Can my staff use it on mobile?**
Yes. Through the Wakflow team inbox, your staff answer from the mobile app or a laptop — several people on
one number, without passing a phone around.

**Is my customer data safe?**
Every number has its own secure key, chats are stored in a private database managed by us, and the whole
system is backed up every night to a separate location. Your data is never shared with other businesses.

**Does it support Hindi and Hinglish?**
Yes. Messages go out in any language, and our AI assistants read and reply in English, Hindi and Hinglish —
including voice notes turned into text.

**What happens if my number gets disconnected?**
We check every number daily. If WhatsApp logs a number out — for example after someone removes the linked
device on the phone — we guide you through re-linking it safely, and your saved history stays intact.

**Can I send offers to all my customers?**
Yes, with Wakflow WhatsApp Broadcasts. For large lists we recommend the official API with approved templates,
which is built for volume.

**Can I connect more than one number?**
Yes — one per branch, department or brand, each with its own inbox, settings and automations.
