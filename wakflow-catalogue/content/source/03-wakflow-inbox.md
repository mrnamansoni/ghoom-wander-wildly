### Replaces

Replaces Freshdesk, Zendesk, Intercom and the WATI or Interakt team inbox — and the one office phone that everybody passes around.

### The problem

1. **"Our WhatsApp lives on one phone."** When the person holding it is busy, on leave or asleep, customers wait. When two people share it, both reply, or neither does.
2. **"I don't know who promised the customer what."** Staff reply from their own phones. There is no history the manager can see, and when an employee leaves, the chats and the customers leave with them.
3. **"Our leads are in five different places."** Instagram DMs, Facebook ad replies, website visitors, email and three WhatsApp numbers. Half of them get a slow reply, and some never get one at all.

### Key benefits

**No missed messages.** Every chat from every channel lands in one list with a clear status: open, pending, snoozed or resolved. Nothing hides in someone's personal phone.

**Clear ownership.** Each conversation is assigned to one person or team. Everyone can see who is handling it, and the manager can see who has not replied.

**Your number, your whole team.** Keep the WhatsApp number your customers already know. Your whole team can answer from it at the same time, from laptops or from the mobile app, and every reply goes out from the business number.

**AI first, people next.** Our AI agent answers routine questions in seconds, day and night, inside the same inbox. When a customer needs a person, the chat is right there for your team with the full history.

**Know how your team is doing.** Live dashboards and reports show volume, first reply time, resolution time, busiest channels, top reasons for contact and customer satisfaction.

**Your data stays yours.** Your conversations run on your own private workspace, backed up every night, and you can export them any time.

### FAQs

**How is it priced?**
A one-time setup fee and a monthly plan based on the number of team members or inboxes. There is no per-message charge on a number linked by QR code. On the official WhatsApp API, Meta's own conversation charges are billed at cost. We share a written quote after a short call.

**How long does setup take?**
A basic inbox with your WhatsApp number and team is usually ready in a day. A full setup with saved replies, labels, teams, rules, a website chat bubble and reports takes a few days. Instagram, Facebook and official WhatsApp connections depend on Meta's approval time.

**Can I keep my existing WhatsApp number?**
Yes. Link your current number by scanning a QR code and your customers keep messaging the number they already have. You can also connect an official WhatsApp Business API number.

**Will my number get banned?**
The inbox only sends what your team or your AI agent writes to customers who are talking to you, which is normal business use. Bans come from bulk messages to people who never contacted you. We guide you on safe practice, and for very high volumes we recommend the official API, which we also connect.

**Can my staff use it on their mobile?**
Yes. There are apps for Android and iPhone with push alerts, so owners and field staff can reply from anywhere. Everything they send goes out from the business number, not their personal one.

**Does it work in Hindi and Hinglish?**
Yes. Customers can write in Hindi, Hinglish, English or any language, and the team screens are available in Hindi and other Indian languages. Our AI agent replies naturally in Hinglish, and messages can be translated inside the chat.

**How many people can use one number?**
As many as you need. Many team members can answer from the same number at the same time, and collision alerts stop two people answering the same customer.

**What happens when an employee leaves?**
You remove their access in one click. The conversations, notes and customer history stay with your business, not in their phone.

**Is my data safe?**
Each business gets its own private workspace, logins can be protected with two-step verification, and your data is backed up every night. You can export your conversations and customer list at any time.

**Can the AI answer and still let my team take over?**
Yes. The AI answers routine questions instantly, and when a customer needs a person the chat is right there in the inbox with the whole history. The senior team gets an alert for important cases.

**Can I see how fast my team replies?**
Yes. Live view shows what is waiting right now, and reports show first reply time, resolution time and volume per person, per channel and per label.

**We already use another helpdesk. Can you move us?**
Yes. We move your data across from tools such as Freshdesk, Zendesk, Intercom and Front, and set up your team the same day you switch.
