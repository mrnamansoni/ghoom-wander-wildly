### The problem

1. **"Leads come in on three WhatsApp numbers, Instagram and the website — and half of them never get
   a call back."** Enquiries sit in different phones and inboxes. Nobody knows who replied, who promised
   a callback, or which lead came from which ad. By the time someone checks, the customer has booked
   with a competitor.
2. **"When a salesperson leaves, their customers leave with them."** Notes live in a personal phone, a
   diary or a private sheet. A new person starts from zero and calls a customer who already said no —
   or never calls one who was ready to buy.
3. **"Every CRM I tried was built for somebody else."** They talk about "deals" and "accounts" while you
   sell trips, flats, admissions or treatments. Staff find it heavy, go back to Excel within a month,
   and you keep paying for software nobody opens.

### Key benefits

**Leads enter the CRM by themselves.**
WhatsApp chats, Instagram DMs, website forms and phone calls become leads automatically, each tagged with
where it came from. Nobody types a phone number twice.

**Nobody forgets a follow-up.**
Every new lead gets a follow-up task with a due date and an owner. Each salesperson opens "My follow-ups
today" and knows exactly who to call.

**Built around your business, not the other way round.**
Your own record types, your own fields and your own stages — Bookings, Site Visits, Students, Patients,
whatever you sell. The CRM speaks your language from day one.

**Each salesperson sees their own leads. You see everything.**
Set who can see, edit or delete what — down to single fields and single records. Interns do not see deal
values; branch staff see only their branch.

**Nothing is ever lost.**
Every change is recorded with its old and new value, and deleted records wait in a recycle bin before they
are removed. A wrong edit can be traced and reversed field by field.

**Your data stays yours.**
Your data is kept in its own separate space on servers we manage, backed up every night, and you can
export all of it whenever you want. No lock-in.

### Features

#### Shaped around your business

- **Ready from day one.** People, companies, deals, tasks and notes are set up before you log in, so your
  team can start working the same day.
- **Your own record types.** Add Bookings, Site Visits, Students, Courses, Patients, Policies, Vehicles or
  Projects as proper records with their own screens, lists and pipelines — not as a notes field.
- **Any field you need.** More than twenty field types: text, long text, numbers, rupee amounts, dates and
  times, dropdowns, multi-select, yes/no, star ratings, phone with country code, email, links, address,
  lists, files and links to other records.
- **Linked records.** Connect a person to a company, a booking to a customer, a student to many courses,
  and see the whole picture from either side.
- **Clean data rules.** Unique fields stop duplicates, default values fill themselves in, rupees and +91
  are the defaults, and an old field can be retired without losing what was stored in it.
- **Your own stages and statuses.** "Follow-up", "Booking confirmed", "Not booked", "Transfer" — the
  words your team already uses, in the order your sale actually moves.
- **No caps.** Unlimited records, record types, fields, views, dashboards and users.

#### Every customer on one page

- **Record pages designed for your team.** Arrange each record page with tabs and blocks: key fields,
  linked records, emails, meetings, timeline, tasks, notes, files, charts, rich text and even an embedded
  web page.
- **Full history timeline.** Every change, email, meeting, note and task appears in time order on the
  record — so the history stays with the customer, not with the salesperson.
- **Notes and tasks.** Rich-text notes and tasks with an owner and a due date, linked to any record.
- **Files on the record.** Keep quotations, KYC documents, agreements, tickets and site photos with the
  customer they belong to.
- **Favourites and instant search.** Pin the records and lists you use most, arrange the sidebar, and press
  Ctrl+K to find anyone or run an action in seconds.
- **Recycle bin.** Deleted records are kept for 14 days (you can change this) and can be restored in one
  click.

#### Views and pipelines

- **Spreadsheet view.** Feels like Excel — edit in the cell, group, hide and reorder columns — but every
  row is a real customer record.
- **Drag-and-drop pipeline boards.** Move leads between your stages on a board, with the total value per
  stage and the time each lead has spent in its stage.
- **Calendar view.** See follow-ups, site visits, departures, classes or renewals on a calendar.
- **Saved filters and personal views.** "My leads", "Today's follow-ups", "Hot leads this week",
  "Bookings above ₹5 lakh" — filters with AND/OR logic, sorting and grouping, saved as one-click views.
  A view can be shared with everyone or kept private.

#### Leads that arrive by themselves

- **Automatic lead capture from every channel.** Every enquiry on your WhatsApp numbers, Instagram DMs and
  website forms becomes a lead with name, phone, travel or visit dates, group size and interest.
- **The source on every lead.** Each lead is tagged with the exact number, page or campaign it came from,
  so you know which channel is worth its money.
- **Updated, not duplicated.** When the same customer writes again, their existing record is updated
  instead of a second copy being created.
- **A follow-up task for every new lead.** The moment a lead is created, a task appears with a due date,
  ready for a salesperson to pick up.
- **Separate lead books for separate brands.** Run two brands in one CRM, each with its own lead list,
  its own team and its own stages, with enquiries routed to the right one automatically.
- **Bookings with payment details.** Website bookings arrive as booking records with order number, trip or
  package, travellers, amounts paid and due, payment status and transaction reference.
- **Every call on the customer's record.** Calls made by the Wakflow voice agent appear on the customer
  with outcome, summary, score and a recording link.
- **Status changes that do the paperwork.** When a salesperson marks a lead "Booking confirmed", the
  booking is logged in your bookings register automatically.

#### Email and calendar

- **Email sync.** Connect Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or any standard mailbox and every email with a
  customer is saved on their record automatically — multiple mailboxes per person, with filters and
  privacy levels.
- **Send email from the CRM.** Write and send emails from inside the customer record, through your own
  mailbox.
- **Calendar sync.** Past and upcoming meetings appear on the right customer's record.
- **Contacts that create themselves.** New people and companies can be added automatically from emails and
  meetings.

#### Automation inside the CRM

- **Triggers on any change.** Start an automation when a record is created, changed (even only when a
  particular field changes) or deleted — on any record type, including your own.
- **One-click buttons.** Give staff a button such as "Send quotation" or "Send payment reminder" that runs
  on one record or many at once.
- **Scheduled jobs.** Daily task summaries, weekly pipeline reports and renewal checks that run on a
  timetable.
- **Receive data from anywhere.** A secure web address that accepts leads from forms, ads and other tools.
- **Create, update, find and merge records.** Automations can create or change records, search with
  filters, and update-if-it-exists-else-create, so leads from many sources never duplicate.
- **Real business logic.** If/else branches, filters, loops over lists of records and waits until a time or
  a date — enough to run follow-up sequences, SLA checks and approvals.
- **Automatic emails.** Welcome emails, reminders and alerts sent from your connected mailbox.
- **Quick forms for staff.** Show a short form in the middle of a process — for example a "Quick Lead"
  form for staff in the field.
- **Connect to any system.** Custom code and web requests let an automation send a WhatsApp, update your
  billing system or call any service with an API.
- **Run history and versions.** See every run step by step, and keep drafts and versions so changes are
  safe.
- **Ready-made recipes.** Task-due alerts, stale-deal detection, closed-won follow-through, auto-replies to
  inbound email, quotations and PDFs.

#### AI in your CRM

- **Ask your CRM in plain English.** "Show bookings above ₹1 lakh closing this month" or "Summarise my

- **AI agents that do the work.** Enrich records, score and classify leads, summarise, draft follow-up

- **Connect your own AI assistant.** Your AI assistant can read and update the CRM directly through a

#### Dashboards

#### Import, export and moving in

- **Excel and CSV import.** Bring in people, companies or any record type from a spreadsheet, with column

- **Export any time.** Download any list whenever you want. Your data is always yours.

#### Team access and security

- **Roles and permissions.** Decide who can see, edit, delete or permanently destroy records — per record

- **Custom CRM apps.** Add your own record types, screens, logic, AI skills and menus as a reusable app.

#### Your brand, your language

- **One workspace per business.** Each business or branch gets its own workspace and its own web address,

### FAQs

**How is Wakflow CRM priced?**
A one-time setup fee for shaping the CRM and moving your data in, then a monthly fee — per user, or one
flat fee for your whole workspace. We quote after the first call, once we know your team and channels.

**How long does setup take?**
Most of the work is configuration, not coding, so a standard CRM is ready in days. Moving years of old
data or connecting a special system adds time, and we tell you exactly how much before we start.

**Can my staff use it on their mobile?**
Yes. It runs in any phone or laptop browser — nothing to install. Salespeople can check their
follow-ups and update leads from their phone between calls.

**Is my customer data safe? Who can see it?**
Your data is kept in its own separate space, backed up every night, and only people you invite can log
in. You decide who sees what, down to single fields and single records.

**What if someone deletes or overwrites something by mistake?**
Deleted records wait in a recycle bin and can be restored in one click. Every change is recorded with its
old and new value, so even a wrong bulk update can be traced and reversed field by field.

**Can it capture leads from my WhatsApp numbers? Will my number get banned?**
Yes — every WhatsApp enquiry becomes a lead automatically. The CRM itself never messages anyone; WhatsApp
sending runs through Wakflow WhatsApp Connect with safe-sending practices, and the official WhatsApp
Business API is available for businesses that want zero ban risk.

**Does it work in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Your team can type names, notes and messages in Hindi, Hinglish or any language. The menus are in
English or 28 other languages, and dates, times and rupee amounts follow Indian formats.

**Can I bring in my Excel sheet or old CRM?**
Yes. We import your sheets or old CRM data, match the columns, remove duplicates and keep the original
dates.

**Can each salesperson see only their own leads?**
Yes. Each person sees the leads assigned to them; managers and the owner see everything.

**Do I need a technical person to run it?**
No. We set it up, connect your channels and look after it. Adding a field or a stage later is a
two-minute job in the settings, and we are there if you need help.

**I run two businesses or several branches. Do I need two CRMs?**
No. Each brand or branch can have its own lead book, team and stages inside one CRM, or its own
separate workspace with its own web address.

**If I leave, can I take my data?**
Yes. Export any list at any time. Your data is always yours.
