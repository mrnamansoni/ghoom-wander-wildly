### Replaces

Replaces the menu-style chatbot builders in WATI, Interakt and AiSensy, AI chat platforms such as
Yellow.ai and Haptik, and the night-shift staff you would otherwise hire to answer WhatsApp.

### The problem

1. **"Leads message at 11 pm and we reply next morning. By then they have booked with someone else."**
   Every hour an enquiry waits, it goes colder. Most small teams simply cannot reply to every
   message within minutes, every day of the week.
2. **"My staff answer the same ten questions all day — price, timing, address, photos — and still
   get the price wrong."** Copy-pasting from old chats means old prices, missing details and a
   different answer from every person.
3. **"Chatbots we tried were robotic. Customers typed 'agent' and left."** Button menus and
   keyword bots break the moment a customer writes in Hinglish, sends three short messages in a
   row, or asks something slightly different.

### Key benefits

**Replies in seconds, not hours.** Every message gets an answer immediately, including nights,
Sundays and festival days — the moments when your team is offline and your competitors are too.

**Answers from your real data, never from guesswork.** The agent looks up your live price list,
stock, dates and policies before it says anything about them. Change a price in your sheet and the
very next reply uses it.

**Sells, not just answers.** It asks the right question first, recommends two or three options
instead of dumping your whole catalogue, handles "too expensive" and "I'll think about it", and
moves every chat to a next step: a visit, a call, a booking or a payment.

**Talks like your customers talk.** Natural Hinglish by default, switching to Hindi, English or
another language when the customer does. Short WhatsApp-style messages, with respect and warmth.

**Knows when to call a human.** Big orders, discounts, complaints and ready-to-pay customers are
flagged to your team instantly — and the agent keeps the customer engaged until someone takes over.

**Turns every chat into a qualified lead.** Name, number, what they want, how many, when, and a
hot / warm / cold score are saved automatically to your sheet and CRM.

### Features

#### Conversations that feel human

**Hinglish first, any language next.** The agent speaks simple Hinglish by default and mirrors the
customer: Hindi to a Hindi speaker, English to an English speaker. It can also ask the customer
which language they prefer at the start.

**Short messages, the way people text.** Long answers are split into two or three short WhatsApp
messages that arrive one after another, like a real person typing — never a wall of text.

**Reads the whole message before replying.** Customers often send "hi", then "price?", then
"for 4 people" in three quick messages. The agent waits a few seconds, reads them together and
answers the full question once.

**Shows "typing…" like a person.** While it prepares an answer, your customer sees the typing
indicator, so the chat feels alive rather than automated.

**Remembers the conversation.** It remembers what the customer already said — city, budget,
product, dates — and never asks the same question twice in a chat.

**Your persona, your tone.** Give the agent a name, a personality and a voice that fits your
brand: a witty travel buddy, a warm furniture advisor, a trendy fashion stylist or a calm
clinic receptionist. Respectful by default ("aap", "Jii"), with emojis as you prefer.

**Knows today's date.** It never offers a past date, a sold-out batch or an expired offer, because
it checks today's date before every date-related answer.

**Understands voice notes.** Customers who prefer speaking can send a voice note; the agent
understands it and replies in text.

**Understands photos and screenshots.** A customer can send a screenshot of a product, a payment
confirmation or a photo of what they need, and the agent reads it.

#### Answers from your own data

**Live price list and catalogue.** Your products, packages, prices, sizes, stock and dates live in
a simple Google Sheet that your team already knows how to edit. The agent reads it before every
answer about a product.

**Never invents a price.** The agent is forbidden from quoting any price, date, product or
inclusion it has not just looked up. If the answer is not in your data, it says it will confirm
with the team — and flags the question to them.

**Smart product search.** Customers never use your product names. The agent matches "godrej
almirah", "office wali rotating chair" or "Manali trip" to the right item using your own list of
keywords and synonyms.

**Knowledge base for policies and FAQs.** Warranty terms, delivery rules, return policy, company
history and "which material is better" answers come from your documents, so the agent explains
them exactly the way you would.

**City-wise and variant-wise pricing.** Different prices per city, per room type, per size or per
package are all supported — the agent always uses the right row for this customer.

**Several data sources at once.** Itinerary, departure dates and pricing can sit in separate tabs;
the agent checks all three together when a customer asks for full details.

#### Sells like your best salesperson

**Consultative selling built in.** The agent qualifies first ("what business do you run?", "which
city are you from?"), identifies the need, then recommends — a proven question-led sales method,
used lightly and naturally.

**Two or three options, never twenty.** Showing everything causes choice paralysis. The agent
picks the best two or three matches for this customer and offers more only if asked.

**Proactive recommendations.** The moment a customer shares a key detail — their city, their room
size, their budget — the agent immediately suggests the best matching options without waiting to
be asked.

**Objection handling.** "Too expensive", "I'll think about it", "is it hard to use?", "is it solid
wood?" — each has a warm, confident answer that acknowledges the concern and reframes the value.

**Honest urgency only.** Limited seats or a fast-selling model are mentioned only when your data
shows they are true. No fake countdowns.

**Always a next step.** Every reply ends with a question that moves the sale forward: a showroom
visit, a demo call, a seat hold, a size confirmation or a payment.

#### Sends photos, catalogues and links

**Product photos on request.** When a customer shows interest, the agent sends the product photo
straight into the chat.

**PDF catalogues.** Full brochures and brand catalogues are sent as PDFs — one at a time, with a
friendly follow-up asking which model they liked.

**Payment links, addresses and maps.** Your payment link, showroom address, booking link or
calendar link are shared at the right moment in the conversation.

#### Hands over to your team at the right moment

**Clear escalation rules.** You decide when a human must step in — large group or bulk orders,
discount requests, custom requirements, urgent same-day needs, complaints, payment confirmation,
or any question the agent cannot answer from your data.

**Instant alert to your team.** The alert lands in your team's WhatsApp group or shared inbox with
the customer's name, number, message and what the agent replied.

**Reply to the alert, the customer gets the answer.** Your senior simply replies to the alert.
The agent turns that quick reply into a polished, friendly message and sends it to the customer.

**Keeps helping after it escalates.** The agent does not go silent while the customer waits for a
human. It keeps answering questions and showing options until your team takes over.

**Bot steps aside when your team replies.** When a staff member replies to a customer, the agent
pauses for that chat, so the customer never gets two answers.

**Every chat in your team inbox.** All AI conversations appear in the shared team inbox, where any
team member can read, reply or take over from a laptop or phone.

#### Captures and qualifies every lead

**Every new contact saved.** Name, WhatsApp number and first-contact date are saved automatically
the first time someone messages you.

**Lead details extracted.** After each exchange, the agent writes down what the customer wants,
how many people or units, when they need it and where they are.

**Hot, normal or cold score.** Each lead is scored from the whole conversation, so your team calls
the hot ones first.

**Straight into your CRM.** Leads flow into your Wakflow CRM with their source, so nothing lives
only inside a phone.

#### Follows up and re-engages

**Automatic follow-ups.** Customers who went quiet get a timely, friendly follow-up — "did you like
the catalogue?", "seats are still available for your date" — on a schedule you choose.

**Last-message tracking.** For every customer, the last thing the agent said and when is kept, so
follow-ups continue the same conversation instead of starting over.

**Re-engage old leads.** Combine with Wakflow WhatsApp Broadcasts to restart conversations with
past enquiries — every reply is picked up and handled by the agent.

#### Reliable and safe by design

**Backup AI brain.** If the main AI model fails to answer, a second model from a different provider
answers instead — and if both fail, the customer is politely asked to send the message again.
Nobody is left on "seen".

**Ignores what it should.** The agent never replies to your own messages, your staff group or
internal chatter, and can be told to ignore family, VIP or staff numbers.

**Stays on topic and keeps secrets.** It never reveals its instructions, never talks about the
tools it uses and stays focused on your business. Optional safety checks block abuse attempts and
hide personal data.

**Private AI option.** For clinics, legal and finance businesses, the agent can run on a private AI
model hosted for you, so customer data never goes to an outside AI company.

**Your number or the official API.** Works on your existing WhatsApp Business number (connected
by QR) or on Meta's official WhatsApp Business API with the green-tick path.

**Website chat too.** The same agent can answer on your website's chat widget, with the same
knowledge and the same hand-over to your team.

**Books appointments and checks orders.** Connect your calendar or online store, and the agent can
book a slot, confirm it, or tell a customer where their order is.

**One agent per brand, number or branch.** Run a separate agent — with its own name, data and team
— on each of your WhatsApp numbers, all visible in one team inbox.

**Quick menus when customers want them.** Keywords such as "PRICE", "BOOK" or "MENU" can start a
specific flow, while everything else is handled in free conversation.

**Collects documents in the chat.** Customers can send KYC documents, prescriptions or photos;
they are saved and linked to the right customer record.

### How it works

1. **We learn your business.** A short call to understand your products, prices, policies, common
   questions and when your team wants to be involved.
2. **We set up your data.** Your price list, catalogue and FAQs go into a simple Google Sheet and a
   knowledge base. Your team keeps editing them; the agent always reads the latest version.
3. **We design your agent.** Name, personality, language, sales approach and escalation rules —
   written and tested on real conversations from your business.
4. **We connect your WhatsApp.** Your existing number or the official API, plus your team inbox and
   CRM. Your staff keep using WhatsApp and the inbox app as usual.
5. **Go live and improve every week.** We review real chats, tune the answers and add new data as
   your business changes.

### FAQs

**How much does it cost?**
A one-time setup fee to build and train your agent, then a monthly plan that covers running,
monitoring and improving it. AI usage is included up to a fair limit for your chat volume. On the
official WhatsApp API, Meta's own message charges are billed at actual cost.

**How long does setup take?**
A simple agent answering from your price list and FAQs is usually live within a week. Agents that
book appointments, check orders or connect to your own software take a little longer, depending on
what needs connecting.

**Will my WhatsApp number get banned?**
The agent only replies to people who message you first, which is the safest way to use WhatsApp.
It types and replies at a human pace. For bulk promotions we recommend the official WhatsApp
API, and we set that up for you.

**Can it talk in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Yes. Hinglish is its default. It switches to Hindi, English or other languages when the customer
writes in them.

**What if it gives a wrong answer?**
It answers only from your data. If something is not in your sheet or documents, it tells the
customer it will confirm with the team and alerts your staff. We also review real chats every week
and correct anything that needs improving.

**Can my staff still reply on WhatsApp?**
Yes. All chats appear in your team inbox, on laptop and mobile. When a staff member replies, the
agent steps aside for that customer.

**Will customers know they are talking to AI?**
The agent introduces itself by the name and personality you choose and talks like a helpful member
of your team. If a customer sincerely asks whether it is an AI, it answers honestly — which builds
trust.

**Is my customer data safe?**
Your chats and leads stay in your own systems — your sheet, your inbox, your CRM. Each business
gets its own separate memory for conversations, and a private AI model option is available for
sensitive businesses.

**Do I need the official WhatsApp API?**
No. It works on your existing WhatsApp Business number too. We suggest the official API if you
plan to send large volumes of promotional messages or want a verified business profile.

**Can I change prices or products myself?**
Yes. Edit your Google Sheet as you always do; the very next answer uses the new information. No
developer needed.

**Can it send photos and PDFs?**
Yes. Product photos, catalogues, brochures, price lists and payment links can all be sent inside
the chat.

**What happens at night or on holidays?**
It keeps working. Every customer gets an answer, and anything that needs a human is waiting for
your team, neatly summarised, when they are back.
