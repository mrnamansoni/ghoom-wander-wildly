Replaces copying numbers from WhatsApp into Excel, "please add this lead" messages to the office, and the

### The problem

1. **"We get enquiries on four WhatsApp numbers, Instagram and the website. Half of them never reach the
   sales sheet."** Someone is supposed to copy every new number into Excel. On a busy day nobody does, and
   the customer who messaged at 11 pm is forgotten by morning.
2. **"I spend lakhs on ads and I still don't know which number or post brings the bookings."** Every lead
   looks the same in the sheet. Nobody wrote down where it came from, so the next month's budget is a guess.
3. **"The same customer is in our system three times, and one of them has the wrong name."** Staff type the
   same person again with a different spelling, or overwrite a good record with a half-filled one. Nobody
   trusts the list, so everyone keeps their own.

### Key benefits

**No lead is typed by hand.**
The moment someone messages, comments, fills a form or books on your website, they appear in your CRM with
their name, phone number and the date — within about a minute.

**You always know where each lead came from.**
Every lead carries its source: which WhatsApp number, Instagram, which website, which partner or creator
sent them. Your reports finally show which channel sells.

**One customer, one record.**
A returning customer updates their existing record instead of creating a new one. Phone numbers are
cleaned to one format, so "+91 98…", "098…" and "98…" are the same person.

**Every lead gets a follow-up task.**
Each new lead is handed to your team as a task due now. Your salespeople open their list and start calling —
nobody has to decide who picks it up.

**Checked every single day.**
Every morning, an automatic audit compares every new lead from every channel with your CRM and tells you
GREEN, AMBER or RED — with the exact lead to recover if anything went wrong.

**Two brands, one system, no mix-ups.**
Run two brands or branches from the same numbers and inbox. Each lead goes to the right lead book
automatically, and one brand's leads never land in the other's list.

### Features

#### Capture from every channel

**Every WhatsApp number.** Each of your WhatsApp numbers becomes its own lead source. A new chat on any
number creates a lead; a returning customer's record is updated instead.

**Instagram DMs and comments.** People who message or comment on your Instagram and share their number
become leads, tagged "Instagram", ready for a call.

**Website enquiry forms.** Enquiry and "call me back" forms on your website send the lead straight to the
CRM — name, phone, destination or product, dates, group size and budget.

**Website bookings and seat holds.** When a customer holds a seat or pays on your website, the booking and
the customer both reach your records at once.

**Partner and creator tracking.** Leads that come through a partner, creator or influencer link are tagged
with that partner's name, so you can pay commissions on facts, not claims.

**Ad-click enquiries.** Enquiries that start from a "Send WhatsApp message" ad on Facebook or Instagram are
captured like any other chat, so your ad spend shows up as real leads.

**Contest and landing-page leads.** Spin-the-wheel offers, contest entries and campaign landing pages drop
their entries into your lead register with the prize or offer they chose.

**Broadcast replies and AI calls.** Customers who reply to a broadcast, or who speak to the AI calling agent,
are recorded with that as their source.

**Any other source you use.** Facebook lead forms, Google Forms, IndiaMART, JustDial, property portals or
your own app — if it can send an enquiry, we can connect it.

#### Clean, trustworthy data

**Phone numbers cleaned to one format.** Spaces, dashes, "+91" and leading zeros are removed, so one person
is always one number. WhatsApp's newer hidden-number contacts are resolved to the real phone number
automatically.

**Update, never duplicate.** Before creating a lead, the system looks for the same phone number. If it
finds one, it updates that record; if not, it creates a new one.

**Bad data blocked at the door.** A lead with no valid phone number can never overwrite an existing
customer. Group chats and your own team's messages are filtered out, so they never become fake leads.

**Names and dates tidied.** Full names are split into first and last name. Dates from forms and sheets are
converted to one date format your CRM understands.

**A fixed list of lead sources.** Sources come from a fixed list, not free typing — so "Insta", "instagram"
and "IG" never become three different channels in your reports.

#### Routing and follow-up

**Multi-brand routing.** Leads for a second brand or branch go to that brand's own lead book, team and
stages, decided automatically from the source.

**Automatic follow-up task.** Every new or returning lead gets a follow-up task due immediately, linked to
the customer, so it shows up in the right salesperson's list.

**Hot-lead details carried over.** What the customer already told the chatbot or the Instagram flow —
group size, travel dates, destination, lead temperature — is saved on the lead, so the salesperson does not
ask again.

**Owner alerts.** Get a WhatsApp or Telegram message for hot leads, big groups or high-value enquiries the
moment they arrive.

#### A register your team already knows

**Live Google Sheet register.** Every lead is also written to a Google Sheet, so the team, the owner and the
accountant can see the day's leads in a format they already use.

**Edit in the sheet, sync to the CRM.** Correct a name or lead type in the register and the CRM record is
updated automatically.

**Exports any time.** Download your leads by date, source or brand whenever you need them.

#### Checked and protected every day

**Daily lead audit.** Every morning every new WhatsApp contact, sheet row and website event is traced to the
CRM. Each lead is marked OK, misrouted, deleted or missing, with the reason.

**Lost-lead recovery list.** If a lead did not make it — the internet dropped, a service was busy — the
audit names it so your team can call that customer the same day.

**Silent-failure detection.** Errors that software normally hides, such as a CRM that was briefly down, are
found and reported instead of passing as "success".

**Overwrite detection.** The audit checks that no customer record was accidentally replaced by another
lead's details.

**Nightly backups.** The CRM is backed up every night, and the lead register keeps its full version
history.

### How it works

1. **We list your lead sources.** Every WhatsApp number, Instagram account, website form, landing page and
   calling campaign — and what each one should be called in your reports.
2. **We connect them.** Each source sends its leads into one register, cleaned and tagged. Nothing changes
   for your customers.
3. **We shape the rules.** Which brand or branch each source belongs to, who gets the follow-up, which leads
   count as hot, and who is alerted.
4. **Leads start arriving.** From day one every enquiry appears in your CRM with its source and a task.
5. **We check it every day.** The daily audit confirms every lead arrived, and we fix any source that stops
   sending before you notice.

### FAQs

**How is it priced?**
A one-time setup fee for connecting your sources and shaping the rules, then a small monthly fee for
running and checking it. The price depends on how many sources and brands you have — we quote after one call.

**How long does setup take?**
A few days for WhatsApp numbers, Instagram and a website form. Each extra source, such as a lead portal or
your own app, adds a day or two. We tell you the exact plan before we start.

**Will my WhatsApp number get banned?**
No. Lead Capture only reads the messages you receive — it does not send anything to your customers. Your
numbers are connected through Wakflow WhatsApp Connect, and the official WhatsApp Business API is available
if you want zero ban risk.

**Do I need to change my website or my ads?**
Usually not. We add one small connection to your website form. Your ads, links and numbers stay the same.

**What happens if the same customer messages again?**
Their existing record is updated, not duplicated. Their new message, name or details are added to the same
customer.

**Can I still see my leads in Google Sheets?**
Yes. Every lead is also written to a Google Sheet register, and corrections you make there flow back into
the CRM.

**Is my customer data safe?**
Your leads stay in your own CRM and your own Google account. Only your team can see them, and everything is
backed up every night.

**What if a lead is lost somewhere?**
That is exactly what the daily audit is for. Every morning it checks each new lead against the CRM and names
anything missing, so your team can call that customer the same day.

**Can it handle two brands or branches?**
Yes. Each brand or branch gets its own lead book and team, and leads are routed automatically by where they
came from.

**Does it work with Hindi and Hinglish names and messages?**
Yes. Names and messages in Hindi, Hinglish or any language are saved exactly as the customer wrote them.

**Can it capture leads from IndiaMART, JustDial or Facebook lead forms?**
Yes. Any source that can send or export an enquiry can be connected, with its own source tag.

**Can my team use it on mobile?**
Yes. Leads and tasks open in the CRM on any phone browser, and the Google Sheet register works in the
Sheets app.
