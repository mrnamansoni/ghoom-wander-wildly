### Features

#### Comment automation

**AI replies to every comment.** Each new comment gets a public reply written for that comment — it reads
the post's caption, answers the question in one or two sentences and points to the DM.

**Private message to every commenter.** Right after the public reply, the commenter receives a private DM
that uses their name, answers their question with one concrete detail and asks one simple question to
start the conversation.

**Knows what the post is about.** The AI reads your post caption, so a comment on your Manali reel gets a
Manali answer, not a generic "check DM".

**Never repetitive.** Replies deliberately vary their opening, structure and emojis, so your comment section
looks human, not botted.

**Ignores your own comments.** Your team's comments and replies are skipped, so the bot never talks to
itself.

**Keyword triggers.** Run "Comment PRICE to get the itinerary" campaigns — a chosen word in the comments
sends a specific DM, brochure or link.

#### DM menus and guided flows

**Welcome menu with buttons.** A new DM gets a personal welcome by name and tap-to-choose buttons: check
packages, work with us, contact us.

**Package carousels.** Swipeable cards with photo, price and "View package" and "Talk to us" buttons —
twelve packages in two carousels in our own setup.

**Quick-reply questions.** Group size and travel dates are asked with one-tap answers ("Solo", "2–5",
"10–25"; the next few dates and months), so customers answer in a second.

**Group departure flow.** People count → travel dates → phone number → the full package list, then a
friendly close. Each answer is saved as they go.

**Custom package flow.** For tailor-made trips, the customer shares their number and a planner calls them to
design the trip.

**Partnership and collaboration flow.** Creators, agencies and partners get a form link and a WhatsApp
button, kept away from the sales queue.

**Contact options.** "Chat on WhatsApp" and "Get a call" buttons, plus a way back to the main menu from
every step.

**Remembers where each person is.** The flow remembers each customer's step for 30 minutes, so a reply
like "2" or "next week" is understood in context.

#### Instant AI call-back

**Number in the DM, call on the phone.** When a customer who asked for a call types their 10-digit number,
the bot confirms "calling you now", and the AI voice agent rings them.

**The caller knows the context.** The call starts with the customer's name and the fact that they enquired
on Instagram, so it is a warm conversation, not a cold call.

**Call status tracked.** Each call request is logged as pending, then completed, so no request is forgotten.

#### Leads and follow-up

**Lead captured automatically.** Every person who engages is recorded with their Instagram name, username
and the date; when they share a number, the lead is marked hot and sent to your CRM.

**Details carried to sales.** Group size and travel dates chosen in the DM go with the lead, so your
salesperson does not ask again.

**Instagram as a tracked source.** Every Instagram lead is tagged "Instagram" (per brand), so you can see
exactly how many leads and bookings Instagram brings.

**Follow-up task for your team.** Each Instagram lead appears in your CRM with a follow-up task.

**Team inbox for Instagram.** Instagram DMs can sit in your Wakflow Inbox next to WhatsApp, so a person
can take over any conversation.

#### Brand and control

**Several accounts, one system.** Each Instagram account gets its own menus, packages, prices, AI tone and
lead book.

**Your words, your packages.** Menus, carousels, prices and the AI's instructions are written for your
business and changed whenever your offers change.

**Official Instagram connection.** Runs through Instagram's official business messaging, using your
professional account.

### How it works

1. **We map your Instagram journey.** Which posts, which questions people ask, which packages or products to
   show, and when to ask for the number.
2. **We connect your Instagram account.** Your professional account is linked through the official
   Instagram login — no password sharing.
3. **We build your flows.** Welcome menu, carousels, quick replies, comment replies and the AI's tone, in
   your words and your language.
4. **Leads flow to your team.** Every lead goes to your CRM with a follow-up task; call requests go to the
   AI voice agent.
5. **We watch and improve.** We check the flows every day, keep the connection alive and update packages as
   your offers change.

### FAQs

**How is it priced?**
A one-time setup fee for building your flows, then a monthly fee per Instagram account. AI replies and
calls have a small usage cost that we include in the plan or bill at cost.

**How long does setup take?**
About a week for a welcome menu, carousels, comment replies and lead capture. Custom flows and AI calling
add a few days.

**Is this safe for my account? Will Instagram block it?**
It uses Instagram's official business messaging through your professional account — the same method large
brands use. We follow Instagram's messaging rules, such as replying within its time window.

**Do I need to share my Instagram password?**
No. You connect the account yourself through Instagram's official login screen. You can disconnect it any
time.

**Can it reply in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Yes. It replies in Hinglish to Hindi and Hinglish comments and in English to English ones, in a friendly,
casual tone you approve.

**Will the replies sound like a robot?**
No. Every reply is written for that specific comment, uses the person's name where it fits and varies its
style — no copy-pasted "check DM".

**Can my team take over a conversation?**
Yes. Instagram DMs can sit in your Wakflow Inbox, where any team member can jump in.

**Can it work for two brands or two accounts?**
Yes. Each account gets its own menus, packages, tone and lead book.

**What happens to the leads?**
They go to your CRM and lead register, tagged "Instagram", with a follow-up task — and call requests go to
the AI voice agent.

**Can I change the packages and prices myself?**
Tell us what changed and we update it the same day; the AI's knowledge and the carousels are edited in one
place.

**Does it work for Facebook too?**
Yes. Facebook page messages and comments can be handled the same way.
