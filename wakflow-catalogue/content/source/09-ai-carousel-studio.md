### Replaces

Replaces the freelance designer you brief every week, the hours spent in design apps, and the
separate tools you use to write captions and pick hashtags.

### The problem

1. **"We know carousels get saved and shared, but one post takes half a day."** Research the topic,
   write the slides, design each one, write the caption, find hashtags, upload in the right order.
   By Thursday the week's post still has not gone out.
2. **"Every designer makes our brand look different."** Colours drift, the logo moves, the tone
   changes from post to post. Followers stop recognising you in their feed.
3. **"I cannot sit at a laptop to approve posts."** The owner is the only person who can say yes,
   and the owner is on the shop floor, on site or on the road. Content waits for a free evening that
   never comes.

### Key benefits

**One message in, a finished carousel out.** Type the topic in a chat on your phone. Slides,
images, caption and hashtags come back to the same chat, ready to check.

**Researched, not made up.** Before writing, the studio searches the web for current facts about
your topic, so a "Did you know?" slide says something true and specific.

**Always on brand.** Your colours, logo, handle, website and voice are built in. Every slide of
every post follows the same template, so your grid looks like one brand, not ten designers.

**You stay in control.** Nothing is posted until you approve it. You pick the best version of each
slide with one tap, approve the caption, and only then say "Post now".

**Posts straight to Instagram.** Approved slides go up as a single swipeable carousel with your
caption, in the right order, and you get a message the moment it is live.

**Speaks your audience's language.** Crisp English for a business audience, high-energy Hinglish
for young travellers — the voice is set per brand and used on every slide and caption.

### Features

#### From idea to slide plan

**Topic from chat.** Send a topic, a destination, a product name or a pain point as a normal chat
message. That single line starts the whole carousel.

**Live web research.** The studio looks up current information on the topic before it writes, so
facts, local details and numbers on the slides are grounded in real sources.

**Proven slide structures.** Every carousel follows a structure built to be saved and shared:
hook, problem, solution, how it works, impact and call to action for a service business; or hook,
contrast, "did you know", hidden gem, adventure, food, culture, budget, squad and booking call for a
travel brand. Your structure is set once and reused on every post.

**Hooks that stop the scroll.** The first slide is written to stop the thumb: short, bold, curious.
Every later slide has its own headline that makes the reader swipe again.

#### Slides that look designed

**AI-designed slide images.** Each slide is generated as a finished image — headline, visuals,
icons and layout together — sized for the Instagram feed.

**Three versions of every slide.** For each slide you get three different designs and pick the one
you like. No back-and-forth with a designer to "try another option".

**Pixel-perfect template slides.** For posts where every word must be exact — prices, steps,
offers — slides are built from a designed template and rendered as crisp 1080×1350 images, with the
text exactly as written.

**Your brand kit on every slide.** Colour palette, logo, watermark, Instagram handle and website are
applied to every slide, every time.

**Up to ten slides per post.** Short six-slide explainers or full ten-slide guides — the length is
set per brand.

#### Captions that sell

**Captions written to be read.** A hook in the first two lines, the pain, the solution in easy
bullet points, and a clear next step — in plain text with a few well-placed emojis.

**Search-friendly wording.** Three or four high-value keywords are worked naturally into the
caption, so the post can be found in Instagram search.

**A hashtag stack in every caption.** Fifteen hashtags in three sets — broad, niche, and local or
brand-specific.

**Comment-to-get calls to action.** The caption can end with "Comment 'FLOW' to get the guide" or
similar, so interested readers raise their hand in the comments — and can be answered automatically.

#### Approve from your phone

**Tap to approve each slide.** Each design arrives in the chat with an "Approve" button. One tap
saves your pick for that slide.

**Caption approval.** The caption arrives as a message with its own "Approve Caption" button.

**Clear confirmations.** After every approval you get a short message telling you what was saved,
and a reminder to say "Post now" when everything is approved.

**"Post now" when you are ready.** Nothing goes out until you type "Post now". Approve over lunch,
publish in the evening.

#### Publishing and records

**One-message publishing to Instagram.** Your approved slides are uploaded in order as one
carousel post with the approved caption, then published to your Instagram business account.

**Published confirmation.** You get a message in the chat as soon as the post is live.

**Daily content log.** Every day's approved slides and caption are saved in a sheet, so you always
know what was prepared and posted, and when.

#### For owners, teams and agencies

**Several brands, one studio.** Each brand gets its own chat bot, brand kit, voice, slide structure
and Instagram account. We run it for our own travel brand and our own agency brand side by side.

**Works for your whole team.** The people you allow in the brand's chat can request carousels, and
the owner keeps the final "Post now".

**Request carousels from WhatsApp.** Prefer WhatsApp? Send the topic to your studio number on
WhatsApp and approve the slides there.

**Ask your AI assistant for a carousel.** If you also use the Wakflow Personal AI Assistant, just
ask it in conversation — "make a 7-slide carousel on why booking direct is cheaper" — and it writes
and renders the slides for you.

### How it works

1. **We set up your brand.** Colours, logo, handle, voice, the slide structure you want and your
   Instagram business account — done once, together with you.
2. **You send a topic.** One line in the chat on your phone.
3. **The studio researches and writes.** It looks up the topic, plans every slide and writes the
   caption and hashtags.
4. **It designs three versions of each slide** and sends them to you with "Approve" buttons.
5. **You approve** your favourite version of each slide and the caption.
6. **You type "Post now".** The carousel is published to Instagram and you get a confirmation.

### FAQs

**How much does it cost?**
A one-time setup for your brand, then a monthly plan that covers running the studio. AI image and
research usage is billed at actual cost or included in a fixed monthly bundle — your choice.

**How long does setup take?**
Usually a few days: we collect your brand kit, agree the slide structure, connect Instagram and make
the first carousel together.

**Do I need a designer or any design skills?**
No. You send a topic and approve what you like. If you already have a designer, they can give us the
templates and the studio will follow them.

**Will it really look like my brand?**
Yes. Your palette, logo, handle and voice are part of every request, and every slide in a post
follows one template. You also pick from three versions of each slide.

**Can it post without my approval?**
No. Every slide and the caption need your tap, and nothing is published until you type "Post now".

**Can my staff use it on mobile?**
Yes — it runs entirely in a chat app on the phone. You decide who can request carousels and who can
publish.

**Can it write in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Yes. The voice is set per brand. Our travel brand's carousels are written in Hinglish for a young
Indian audience.

**Which platforms does it post to?**
Instagram business accounts are connected on day one. The same 4:5 slides also work for LinkedIn
and Facebook carousels.

**Is my Instagram account safe?**
Posts are published through Instagram's official API for business accounts, the same way
professional scheduling tools do it. You can revoke access from Instagram at any time.

**Are the facts on the slides correct?**
The studio researches the topic on the web before writing, and you review every slide before it goes
out. For exact prices and offers we use template slides so the text is exactly what you gave us.

**Who owns the content?**
You do. The slides and captions are yours to use anywhere.
