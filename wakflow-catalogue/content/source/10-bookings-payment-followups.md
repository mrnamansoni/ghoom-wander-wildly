### The problem

1. **"The customer said yes, but the booking only reached the register three days later."** A salesperson
   confirms on the phone, promises to "update it later", and the operations team finds out when the customer
   calls asking about pickup.
2. **"Half our follow-up time is chasing payments."** Advances, balances, fees, renewals — every one is a
   manual call or a message someone forgot to send. Money that is owed simply stays owed.
3. **"People book and don't turn up."** Appointments, trips, classes and site visits are missed because
   nobody reminded the customer the day before.

### Features

#### Booking capture

**Online seat holds and bookings.** When a customer holds a seat or pays on your website, the booking is
recorded with the order number, package, date, travellers, room sharing and every amount.

**"Booking confirmed" from your CRM.** When a salesperson marks a lead "Booking confirmed" in the CRM, the
booking is added to the bookings register with the customer, destination, group size, departure date, the
salesperson's name and remarks.

**WhatsApp booking bot.** Customers pick a service, date and time slot on WhatsApp and get an instant
confirmation — no calls, no back-and-forth.

**Appointment and slot calendar.** Bookings go into your Google Calendar with the customer's details, so
staff see the day's appointments on their phone.

**Human approval where you want it.** For high-value or custom bookings, a manager approves with one tap on
WhatsApp before the confirmation goes to the customer.

**Contest and offer sign-ups.** Spin-the-wheel offers, festive contests and offer pages record each entry
with the reward chosen, ready for your team to convert into a booking.

#### Payments

**Online payment with UPI and cards.** Take a token, seat hold, advance or full payment online through your
own payment gateway; the booking is marked paid only when the gateway confirms it.

**Payment links on WhatsApp.** Send a payment link for the exact amount due, to the exact customer, from
your business WhatsApp number.

**Payment status on every booking.** Seat held, advance paid, fully paid, completed, cancelled or refunded —
visible to sales, operations and accounts in one place.

**Advance-collected tick.** Your team marks when the full advance has been collected, so everyone knows which
bookings are safe and which still need a call.

**Coupons and discounts recorded.** The coupon code and discount on each booking are saved, so you can see
which offers actually sell.

#### Reminders and follow-ups

**Booking confirmation message.** The moment a booking or payment is confirmed, the customer gets a
WhatsApp confirmation with the details and next steps.

**Payment-due reminders.** Automatic reminders before an advance, balance, fee or EMI is due — with the amount
and a payment link — that stop as soon as the customer pays.

**Appointment and trip reminders.** Reminders a day and an hour before (or at the times you choose), with
location, timing and a "reschedule" option.

**Pre-departure and pre-visit information.** Packing lists, documents to carry, pickup points or clinic
instructions sent automatically before the date.

**Renewal reminders.** Membership, policy, AMC, subscription and course renewals reminded 30, 7 and 1 days
before expiry, with a renewal link.

**Feedback and review requests.** After the trip, visit or service, customers are asked for feedback, and
happy ones get your Google review link.

**Missed-payment escalation.** If a reminder is ignored, the booking is flagged to your team with a call task
— no payment slips through quietly.

#### Invoices and records

**Branded PDF invoice.** A clean invoice with your letterhead, payment summary, amount in words and your
terms — downloadable by the customer and by your team.

**Invoice on WhatsApp.** The invoice or receipt is sent to the customer's WhatsApp as soon as they pay.

**Bookings register in Google Sheets.** Every booking is also written to a sheet your accounts and
operations teams already use.

**Daily bookings summary.** The owner gets a short message every evening: new bookings, money collected,
money still due.

**Every booking checked daily.** An automatic check confirms that every online booking reached both the
register and the CRM.

### How it works

1. **We map your booking journey.** Where bookings happen (website, WhatsApp, phone), what you collect and
   when, and which reminders you want.
2. **We connect your booking sources.** Your website, CRM and payment gateway send each booking and payment
   into one register.
3. **We set up your messages.** Confirmation, reminder, payment-link, invoice and renewal messages in your
   words — in English, Hindi or Hinglish.
4. **Follow-ups run by themselves.** Reminders go out on schedule and stop when the customer pays or
   confirms.
5. **You see everything.** Bookings, amounts collected and amounts due in your CRM and register, with a
   daily summary on your phone.

### FAQs

**How is it priced?**
A one-time setup fee for connecting your booking sources and writing your messages, then a monthly fee.
WhatsApp and payment-gateway charges, if any, are paid directly by you.

**How long does setup take?**
Booking capture and a bookings register take a few days. Reminders, payment links and renewals add a few
days more, depending on how many message types you want.

**Which payment gateways do you support?**
Razorpay, PhonePe, Cashfree, PayU, Paytm, Instamojo and UPI links — any gateway that offers payment links
or payment notifications. The money goes straight into your own account.

**Will reminder messages get my WhatsApp number banned?**
Reminders go only to customers who booked with you, and they are sent at a safe pace. For high volumes we
recommend the official WhatsApp Business API, where reminders use approved templates with zero ban risk.

**Do reminders stop when the customer pays?**
Yes. As soon as the payment is confirmed, the pending reminders for that booking are cancelled.

**Can customers book appointments on WhatsApp themselves?**
Yes. The booking bot shows available slots, confirms the chosen one and puts it in your calendar.

**Can I send a GST tax invoice?**
We create branded invoices with your details and payment summary. For GST tax invoices with your GSTIN and
tax split, we set up the format for your business or connect to your accounting software.

**Can my staff see and update bookings on mobile?**
Yes. Bookings are in your CRM and register, both of which work on any phone.

**Can messages go in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Yes. Every confirmation, reminder and payment message can be written in Hindi, Hinglish or English.

**What if a customer pays in cash or by bank transfer?**
Your team marks it paid in the CRM or register, and the reminders for that booking stop.

**Will I know how much money is still due?**
Yes. Every booking shows the amount paid and still due, and the daily summary tells you the total
outstanding.
