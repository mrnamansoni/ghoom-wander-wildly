### The problem

1. **"We have thousands of old enquiries on WhatsApp and nobody follows them up."** Past leads and
   customers are the cheapest sales you will ever make, but sending to them one by one takes days.
2. **"When we tried bulk sending, the number got restricted."** Identical messages fired off in
   seconds look exactly like spam to WhatsApp — and to customers.
3. **"The broadcast went out, 60 people replied, and we answered them the next day."** A broadcast
   creates a rush of replies. If nobody answers quickly, the interest you paid for disappears.

### Key benefits

**Every message feels personal.** Your offer is rewritten into many different versions in your
brand's voice — in Hinglish, Hindi or English — so it reads like a note from a person, not one
identical blast.

**Sent at a human pace.** "Typing…" before every message and natural, random gaps between them —
the way a real person sends messages, not a machine.

**Every reply answered in seconds.** Replies go straight to your AI WhatsApp Agent or your team
inbox, so interest turns into conversations while it is still warm.

**Opt-outs respected automatically.** "Stop", "unsubscribe" or "no more" gets an instant polite
confirmation, and that person is not messaged again.

**Replies become leads.** Everyone who responds is saved to your sheet and CRM with the source
"Bulk Message", so you know exactly what each broadcast produced.

**Official API when you need scale.** For large campaigns, we set up Meta's official WhatsApp API
with approved templates, buttons and delivery tracking.

### Features

#### Lists and audiences

**Send to a simple sheet.** Your recipient list is a Google Sheet — paste numbers, names and any
detail you want to use in the message. No new software to learn.

**Audiences from your CRM and inbox.** Pick past enquiries, customers who bought, leads of a
certain type, or contacts with a certain label, and send only to them.

**Clean numbers before sending.** Numbers are checked to see which ones actually use WhatsApp, so
you never waste messages on dead numbers.

**Per-recipient status.** The sheet records, against each number, the exact message that was sent
to that person.

#### Personalised messages

**A fresh message for every person.** You write one base message. For each recipient, the AI
rewrites it in your brand's tone with different wording, and near-duplicates are rejected, so your
list never receives one copy-pasted text. Your price, dates, links and opt-out line are always kept
exactly as you wrote them.

**Your voice, your language.** Friendly Hinglish, polished English or simple Hindi — with emojis
as you like. The persona matches the one your customers already know from your chats.

**Name and detail personalisation.** Use the customer's name, city, last enquiry or any column in
your list inside the message.

**Photos, videos and PDFs.** Send a product photo, a short video, a brochure or a price list with
a caption.

**Buttons, lists and carousels.** "Book now", "Call us", "Show details" buttons, option lists and
swipeable product cards for richer campaigns.

**Polls.** Ask a quick question — "Which trip should we plan next?" — and receive every vote as
data.

**WhatsApp Status updates.** Post daily offers or new arrivals to your WhatsApp Status for all
your contacts to see.

**Group announcements.** Post to your customer or dealer groups with one message, mentioning
everyone when it matters.

#### Safe, human-paced sending

**"Typing…" before every message.** Each recipient sees the typing indicator for a few seconds
before your message arrives, exactly as if someone were writing to them.

**Random gaps between messages.** Messages go out with natural, varied gaps instead of a burst,
so sending looks like a person working through a list.

**Only to people who know you.** We design every broadcast for customers and enquiries who have
already talked to you or agreed to hear from you — the safest and most effective audience.

**Daily limits and warm-up.** New numbers start slowly and grow their daily volume step by step;
every campaign has a daily ceiling.

**Scheduled campaigns.** Choose the day and time; the campaign starts on its own and stops at the
end of the sending window.

#### Replies handled instantly

**Every reply gets an instant answer.** When someone replies, they get an immediate, friendly
response — and the conversation continues with your AI WhatsApp Agent, which knows your prices
and packages.

**Replies land in your team inbox.** Your team sees every broadcast reply alongside all other
chats and can take over at any time.

**Opt-out detection.** "Stop", "unsubscribe", "cancel", "remove me", "don't text", "no more" and
similar replies are recognised automatically. The person gets a polite confirmation and is kept
off future broadcasts.

**Responders become leads.** Name and number of everyone who replies are saved with the source
"Bulk Message" and flow into your CRM for follow-up.

#### Official WhatsApp API campaigns

**Approved templates.** We write your marketing and utility templates and get them approved by
Meta.

**Campaigns to labels and segments.** Send a template campaign to a label or saved segment from
your team inbox, with replies arriving in the same inbox.

**Delivery and read tracking.** See which messages were sent, delivered and read.

**Transparent Meta charges.** Meta's per-message charges are billed at actual cost — no hidden
mark-up surprises.

#### Reports

**Campaign report.** How many were sent, how many replied, how many opted out and how many became
leads — per broadcast.

### How it works

1. **Choose your audience.** Past enquiries, customers, a CRM segment or a sheet you already have.
2. **Write one base message.** We help you shape the offer, the call to action and the opt-out
   line. Add a photo, brochure or buttons if you like.
3. **Pick the sending path.** Your existing WhatsApp number for warm lists at a human pace, or the
   official WhatsApp API with approved templates for larger campaigns.
4. **Send.** Each person gets a freshly written message, with "typing…" and natural gaps.
5. **Replies are answered.** Your AI agent and team pick up every reply; opt-outs are honoured;
   responders become leads in your CRM.

### FAQs

**Will my number get banned?**
Sending to people who already know you, at a human pace, with a different message for each person
and an easy way to opt out is the safest way to broadcast on WhatsApp. We never send to purchased
or cold lists. For large volumes we move you to the official WhatsApp API.

**How many messages can I send in a day?**
It depends on your number's age, history and audience. New numbers start with a small daily volume
and grow over a couple of weeks. On the official API, Meta sets your daily limit and raises it as
your quality stays high.

**What does it cost?**
A setup fee, then a monthly plan based on your sending volume. On the official API, Meta's own
per-message charges are billed at actual cost.

**Can I send photos, PDFs and buttons?**
Yes. Photos, videos and PDF brochures work on both paths; buttons, lists and product cards work
best on the official API.

**Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?**
Yes, for warm lists of people who know you. For larger campaigns or cold-ish audiences we recommend
a separate number on the official API.

**What happens when someone replies?**
They get an instant, friendly answer and the conversation continues with your AI WhatsApp Agent or
your team, in your team inbox.

**What if someone says "stop"?**
They get a polite confirmation straight away, and they are kept off future broadcasts.

**Can messages be in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Yes — Hinglish, Hindi or English, in the tone you choose.

**Can I see results?**
Yes. Each broadcast shows how many were sent, replied, opted out and became leads.

**Do I need to approve every message?**
You approve the base message and the offer. The AI only changes the wording, not the facts —
price, dates and links stay exactly as you wrote them.
