### The problem

1. **"We sent 500 emails and got two replies — both asking us to stop."** Bulk tools blast the
   same email from a brand-new account. Mailbox providers see exactly that pattern and send it
   to spam, so most of your list never even sees it.
2. **"Our main domain got blacklisted and now our invoices go to spam."** Sending cold email from
   the domain your customers and accountant rely on is the fastest way to damage it.
3. **"Someone replied 'interested' and we saw it four days later."** Replies get buried among
   out-of-office notes, bounces and "remove me" messages. By the time anyone looks, the prospect
   has moved on.

### Key benefits

**Lands in the inbox.** Every sending account is warmed up for two weeks before it sends a single
cold email, and the daily volume climbs slowly over seven weeks. Inbox providers see a real,
trusted mailbox — not a new account blasting a list.

**Your main domain stays safe.** Cold email goes out from separate sending domains and inboxes.
If one ever gets a bad reputation, it is replaced; your real business email is never touched.

**It stops itself before damage is done.** Every night the engine checks each account's spam,
bounce and reply rates. An account that looks unhealthy is paused automatically, and you get a
WhatsApp message saying which one and why.

**Every reply caught in minutes.** Replies are checked every ten minutes. A real reply stops the
sequence for that person and sends you a WhatsApp alert straight away. Bounces and "no, thanks"
are handled without you.

**Emails that read like a person wrote them.** Plain text, a real name, short and specific to the
prospect's business and city — with several wordings rotated so no two prospects get an
identical email.

**Clean lists only.** Every address is checked before it is used, duplicates and broken rows are
removed, and anyone who said "no" is never emailed again.

### Features

#### Sending accounts and domains

**Separate sending domains.** We set up look-alike domains for outreach (for example a "get" or
"try" version of your brand) that redirect to your main website, so your real domain never sends
cold email.

**Real inboxes with real names.** Each sending inbox is a named person with a photo and signature
— not "info@" or "sales@", which mailbox providers filter harder.

**Email authentication set up for you.** We configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC on every sending domain
and check that all three pass before any inbox is used.

**Per-inbox daily ceilings.** Each inbox has its own hard daily limit that no schedule can
exceed, so adding more inboxes is how volume grows — safely.

**Add inboxes in minutes.** Growing from five inboxes to fifteen is a configuration change, not a
rebuild.

#### Warm-up

**Automatic warm-up network.** Your inboxes email each other every day with natural, AI-written
conversations on everyday business topics — so each one builds a history of real, two-way mail.

**Replies in the same thread.** Warm-up emails are answered inside the same conversation, a
random number of times, the way real colleagues write back and forth.

**Opened, marked important and starred.** Received warm-up mail is opened, marked important,
sometimes starred and filed away — the signals mailbox providers read as "this sender is
wanted".

**Rescued from spam.** If a warm-up email ever lands in spam, it is moved back out within ten
minutes and counted — the single strongest trust signal there is.

**Human timing.** Warm-up runs only in daytime hours, with random skips and random gaps, so it
never looks like a machine on a timer.

**Every email genuinely different.** Each warm-up email gets a random topic and a random opening
style, so the network never repeats itself.

#### Leads and list quality

**Import from a Google Sheet.** Drop your prospect list into a sheet — email, name, company, city,
industry — and the engine takes it from there.

**Cleaning and de-duplication.** Blank, broken and duplicate addresses are removed before import.
A column with a different heading ("E-mail", "Email Address") is still recognised.

**Email verification.** Every address is checked with a professional verification service.
Only addresses confirmed as deliverable are ever emailed; risky, invalid and unknown ones are set
aside.

**Catch-all handling.** Addresses on domains that accept everything are treated separately and
tested in small batches, so they cannot drag down your bounce rate.

**Re-checks old lists.** Addresses verified more than a set number of days ago are re-verified
before they are used again.

**Do-not-email lists.** Your own domains, your clients' domains and competitors can be blocked
completely, alongside every person who ever opted out or bounced.

**Leads from Wakflow Lead Finder.** Prospect lists built by our Lead Finder flow straight in.

#### Campaigns and sequences

**Three-step sequences.** A first email and two follow-ups, spaced days apart, that stop the
moment the prospect replies.

**Follow-ups in the same thread.** Follow-ups arrive as replies to your first email, so the
prospect sees one tidy conversation — exactly as a salesperson would do it by hand.

**Rotating subjects and wording.** Each step has several subjects and bodies, with small word
choices rotated inside them, so emails vary naturally from prospect to prospect.

**Personalised to each prospect.** Name, company, city and industry are filled in for every
email, with graceful fallbacks, so a missing column never produces "Hi ," or a double space.

**Plain text, no tricks.** No images, no links in the first email and no tracking pixels. Emails
look like one person writing to another — which is exactly why they reach the inbox.

**A polite opt-out in every email.** Every email ends with a simple line: reply "no" and we will
not email you again.

**Industry-matched campaigns.** Run a different campaign for each industry you sell to; each
prospect automatically gets the campaign written for their trade.

**Ready starter campaigns.** Proven-structure sequences for travel agencies, clinics and real
estate to adapt, rather than starting from a blank page.

**Hindi, Hinglish or English.** Write your campaign in the language your prospects use.

#### Safe, human-paced sending

**A seven-week ramp.** New inboxes send nothing cold for two weeks, then three a day, climbing
gradually to the daily ceiling by week seven.

**Business hours only.** Cold email goes out Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm India time, never
on Sundays or at night.

**Random gaps and skipped rounds.** Sends happen at random moments inside the window, with
random pauses before each one, and each day's quota varies a little — no fixed rhythm.

**One prospect, one sender.** Every follow-up comes from the same inbox that sent the first
email, so the thread stays consistent.

**Never sent twice.** The engine makes it impossible to send the same step to the same person
twice, even if something is retried.

#### Replies, bounces and opt-outs

**Reply detection every ten minutes.** New replies to your campaigns are picked up throughout
the day.

**Instant WhatsApp alert for real replies.** When a prospect writes back, you get a WhatsApp
message with who it is, so someone can respond while they are still at their desk.

**Sequence stops on any reply.** A reply of any kind ends that prospect's sequence immediately —
no awkward follow-up after they have already answered.

**Opt-outs honoured forever.** "No", "unsubscribe" or "remove me" puts the address on the
do-not-email list permanently.

**Bounces handled.** Bounced addresses are identified, marked and never tried again.

**Out-of-office aware.** An automatic "I'm on leave" reply does not end the sequence; the next
email simply waits a few extra days.

**AI reply sorting.** Replies are sorted into interested, not interested, opt-out, out of office
and wrong person, so the hot ones rise to the top.

**Interested replies to your CRM.** Prospects who reply with interest are created as leads in
your CRM with the source "Cold Email", ready for your sales team.

#### Protection and monitoring

**Nightly health guard.** Each inbox's last seven days are checked every night. Too much spam,
too many bounces, or thirty-plus sends with zero replies pauses that inbox's cold sending for a
few days — warm-up keeps running so it can recover.

**Weekly inbox placement test.** Once a week, each inbox sends one real campaign-style email to a
test mailbox, and the engine records whether it landed in Inbox, Promotions or Spam. Spam pauses
that inbox automatically.

**One broken login pauses only one inbox.** If an inbox's login stops working, that inbox is
paused for a day and you are told — every other inbox carries on.

**Error alerts on WhatsApp.** If any part of the engine fails, you get a WhatsApp message naming
the step and the error.

**Emergency stop.** Every inbox can be paused in one step if you ever need to stop all sending.

#### Reports

**Daily WhatsApp report.** Every evening at 10 pm: per inbox, warm-up sent and received, spam
rescued, cold emails sent, replies, bounces and opt-outs, plus seven-day spam and reply rates —
with anything that needs attention at the top.

**Health at a glance.** Seven-day health for every inbox — spam rate, bounce rate, reply rate and
last placement result — on request at any time.

**Nightly backup.** All campaign data, leads and history are backed up every night to secure
off-server storage.

### How it works

1. **We set up your sending domains and inboxes.** Separate from your main domain, fully
   authenticated, each with a real name and signature.
2. **Warm-up runs for two weeks.** Your inboxes build trust by emailing each other, replying,
   and rescuing anything that lands in spam.
3. **You give us your list — or we build one.** Your prospects go into a sheet, or our Lead
   Finder builds the list for you. Every address is cleaned and verified.
4. **We write the campaign with you.** Three short, plain-text steps in your voice, with several
   wordings for each.
5. **Cold sending starts small and ramps up.** A few emails a day per inbox, rising over the
   following weeks, only in business hours.
6. **Replies come to you.** Interested prospects trigger a WhatsApp alert and land in your CRM;
   opt-outs and bounces are handled automatically; a daily report shows exactly how it is going.

### FAQs

**Will my business domain get blacklisted?**
No — your main domain never sends cold email. We use separate sending domains that redirect to
your website. If one ever picks up a bad reputation, it is retired and replaced.

**How many emails can I send a day?**
Each inbox sends up to 25–30 cold emails a day once fully ramped. More volume comes from more
inboxes, not from pushing one inbox harder. Five inboxes give about 125 a day; twelve give about
360.

**How long before the first cold email goes out?**
About two weeks of warm-up, then a gentle start of three emails a day per inbox, reaching full
volume in week seven. Rushing this is the most common reason cold email fails.

**Do you track opens and clicks?**
We deliberately do not add tracking pixels or links to the first email, because they push emails
towards spam. We measure what actually matters: replies, interested replies and meetings.

**Where do the leads come from?**
From your own list in a Google Sheet, or from our Lead Finder, which builds targeted lists of
decision makers. Every address is verified before it is used.

**Is cold email legal in India?**
Business-to-business cold email is widely used in India. We keep it respectful: relevant
businesses only, a real sender name, no misleading subject lines, and an easy opt-out that is
honoured permanently. We also handle your prospect data carefully under India's data protection
law.

**What happens when someone replies?**
Their sequence stops at once and you get a WhatsApp alert. Interested replies go into your CRM.
"No thanks" and "remove me" are added to the do-not-email list automatically.

**Can the emails be in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Yes. Campaigns can be written in English, Hindi or Hinglish, whichever your prospects respond to.

**Do I need to buy Google Workspace?**
For serious volume, yes — a few inboxes on your sending domains, which we set up for you. The
domain and inbox costs are small and billed at actual cost.

**What does it cost?**
A one-time setup fee (domains, inboxes, authentication, warm-up, first campaign), then a monthly
fee based on the number of sending inboxes. Domain, inbox and verification charges are passed
through at cost.

**Can my team see how it is going?**
Yes. A daily WhatsApp report shows every inbox's numbers, and interested replies are visible in
your CRM.

**What if something breaks?**
You get a WhatsApp alert naming the problem, an inbox with a login problem pauses on its own, and
our team fixes it — usually before you would notice.
