### The problem

1. **"My sales team spends the whole morning searching, not selling."** Finding the right person
   at the right company, then hunting for an email address, eats hours for every handful of
   prospects.
2. **"We bought a database and half the emails bounced."** Ready-made lists are old, generic and
   shared with every competitor who bought the same file.
3. **"We don't know which companies need us right now."** A company that is hiring, expanding or
   posting jobs in your area is ready to buy — but nobody on your team is watching for it.

### Key benefits

**Ask in plain words.** Describe who you want — job title, industry, city, how many — in a chat
message. No filters to learn, no search syntax.

**Decision makers, not generic inboxes.** Lead Finder looks for named people in the roles that
buy: founders, directors, HR heads, marketing and operations managers.

**Work emails found and checked.** For each person it searches several sources for a work email,
cleans it, and checks it before it reaches your list.

**Companies that need you right now.** Job posts are a buying signal. Lead Finder watches new
job posts in your field and city, and tells you which companies are hiring — and who posted the
job.

**First message drafted for you.** Each lead comes with a short, personalised opening message
written from what the company is doing — ready for you to review and send.

**Straight into your outreach.** Lists land in a Google Sheet, your CRM or our Cold Email Engine,
without anyone copying rows.

### Features

#### Ask for leads in a chat

**Request leads on Telegram or WhatsApp.** Send a message like "find 30 CEOs in fintech in
Mumbai". Lead Finder understands the title, industry, city and number you asked for.

**Plain-language understanding.** Plurals, abbreviations and loose wording are turned into a
precise search automatically ("CEOs" becomes "CEO", "Bombay" still means Mumbai).

**Results sent back in the chat.** Profile links arrive in the same conversation as they are
found, and the full list is saved to your sheet.

**Any size of list.** Ask for ten or several hundred; the search pages through results until it
has the number you asked for.

#### People and company search

**Search by title, industry and location.** Target exactly the roles that buy what you sell, in
the cities you serve.

**Professional profile links.** Every lead comes with a link to the person's public professional
profile, so your team can see who they are before reaching out.

**Company research.** For each company, Lead Finder reads the company's website and public
information to understand what it does — useful for qualifying and for personalising.

**Top contacts per company.** Give it a company name and it finds the three people most worth
contacting there, such as the HR head, the hiring manager and a senior leader.

**Named-account lists.** Upload a list of target companies and receive the key people at each.

#### Buying signals from job posts

**New job posts in your market.** Lead Finder collects recent job posts for chosen keywords and
cities — a company hiring salespeople, drivers or accountants is growing and has budget.

**Who posted the job.** Where a job post names the person who posted it, that person is captured
as a warm, relevant contact.

**Jobs split by contact found.** Posts with a named poster and posts without one are kept in
separate lists, so your team starts with the easiest wins.

**Company details with every post.** Company name, size and website come along with each job.

#### Email finding and checking

**Several sources, one answer.** For each person, Lead Finder tries more than one email-finding
source, and also reads the job post and the company website for a published address.

**Email cleaning.** Stray characters, spaces and formatting errors are removed so every address
is usable.

**Verification before use.** Addresses are checked for deliverability before they go into a
campaign, protecting your sending reputation.

**No duplicates.** A person or address already on your list is not added again.

#### Personalised first messages

**A first message for each lead.** Lead Finder writes a short, natural opening email or LinkedIn
note based on the person's role and what their company is doing.

**Draft, never auto-send.** Messages are saved as drafts in your mailbox. You read, edit and send
— nothing goes out in your name without you.

**Your tone and language.** Friendly or formal, English or Hinglish, short or detailed.

#### Recruitment edition

**Hiring-signal leads for staffing firms.** Find companies hiring in your specialism and the HR
and talent people behind the posts.

**Candidate and talent search.** Find professionals by title, skill and city for your open roles.

**Job-seeker assistant.** For candidates and career-services businesses: finds matching jobs,
tailors the resume to each job, creates a polished resume PDF, finds the hiring contact and drafts
a cover-letter email.

**Profile-based resume building.** Builds a structured resume from a professional profile or a
developer's code portfolio, then tailors it to each job's keywords.

#### Delivery and follow-through

**Google Sheet delivery.** Every list lands in a shared sheet your team can filter and work
through.

**CRM delivery.** Leads can be created in your CRM with the source "Lead Finder" and the right
owner.

**Hand-off to cold email.** A verified list flows straight into a Cold Email Engine campaign.

**Scheduled searches.** Run the same search every week and receive only the new people and new
job posts.

### How it works

1. **Tell us your ideal customer.** Roles, industries, cities, company size — and what you sell.
2. **Ask for leads.** Send a chat message, upload a company list, or let a weekly search run on
   its own.
3. **Lead Finder searches.** It finds people and job posts, reads company websites and looks up
   work emails from several sources.
4. **The list is cleaned and checked.** Duplicates removed, emails tidied and verified.
5. **First messages are drafted.** One personalised opener per lead, saved as a draft.
6. **You reach out.** From your mailbox, your CRM, or an automatic Cold Email Engine campaign.

### FAQs

**Where do the leads come from?**
From public professional profiles, public job posts, company websites and professional
email-finding services. We do not sell you a recycled database.

**How many leads can I get?**
As many as your market has. Most businesses start with 50–200 well-targeted leads a week — enough
to follow up properly.

**Are the emails accurate?**
Each email is looked up from several sources, cleaned and checked for deliverability before it
reaches your campaign. Some people simply have no public work email; those are marked so your team
can use another route.

**Will you send messages on my behalf?**
Only if you want. By default the first messages are saved as drafts for you to review. For
automatic outreach, we connect Lead Finder to our Cold Email Engine.

**Can I ask for leads in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Yes. Write your request the way you speak — the chat understands it.

**Is this legal?**
We use public professional information for business-to-business outreach, keep a record of where
each lead came from, and make sure every outreach message offers an easy way to opt out. We handle
the data carefully under India's data protection law.

**Can I target a specific list of companies?**
Yes. Share the company names and Lead Finder returns the key people at each.

**How fast is it?**
A chat request returns its first results within minutes. Larger research lists with company
details and emails are usually ready the same day.

**What does it cost?**
A setup fee, then a monthly plan based on how many leads you need. Paid data sources used for
email finding are included up to your plan's limit.

**Can my team use it on mobile?**
Yes. Requests and results work in Telegram or WhatsApp on any phone, and the list is a Google
Sheet anyone can open.
