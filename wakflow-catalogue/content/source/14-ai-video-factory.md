### Replaces

Replaces the scriptwriter, voice artist, video editor and caption tool you would otherwise hire or

### The problem

1. **"Everyone says post a Short every day. We cannot even post one a week."** Writing, recording,
   editing, captioning and exporting one 60-second video takes hours. Daily posting needs a team.
2. **"We do not want to be on camera."** Many owners and creators want a channel that grows without
   showing their face or recording their own voice.
3. **"Our videos all look the same, or we run out of ideas."** After ten videos the ideas dry up and
   topics start to repeat — and the audience notices.

### Key benefits

**Never repeats a topic.** The factory keeps a list of everything it has already made and picks a

**Turn any idea into cinematic clips.** Type a scene in one line and get several eight-second AI video
clips with sound to choose from — for ads, reels and story openers.

### Features

#### Story shorts on autopilot

**Fresh topic every time.** The factory checks your list of past videos and invents a new, original
title that has not been done before.

**Story scripts that hold attention.** Each script follows a proven shape — a hook in the first
line, a setup, rising tension or a problem, and a satisfying ending with a call to follow.

**Hindi voice with Hinglish captions.** The voice-over is written in Hindi for a natural sound, and a
matching Hinglish version in English letters is used for the on-screen captions, so everyone can read
along.

**Scene-by-scene visuals.** Every scene gets its own AI image in one consistent visual style, so the
video looks like one story, not a slideshow of random pictures.

**Consistent characters.** For children's stories, the main character is described once and drawn
the same way in every scene.

**Gentle camera motion.** Each image is animated with slow zooms and pans, timed exactly to the
length of its line of voice-over.

**Word-by-word captions.** Bold, high-contrast captions appear a few words at a time, timed to the
voice — how most people watch Shorts, with the sound off.

**Background music.** A music bed is mixed under the voice at the right level.

**Vertical HD export.** Every video is exported in 1080×1920 for Shorts and Reels.

#### Ready-made formats

**Horror and true-crime shorts.** Chilling one-minute Hindi stories with a deeper, eerie narrator
voice, dark cinematic visuals and a twist ending.

**Kids' moral stories.** Fifteen-scene animated stories for children aged 4–10, with a friendly
character, a simple problem, a happy ending and a moral.

**Longer story videos.** 25-scene stories for viewers who want more than a minute.

**Your own format.** Business tips, product stories, travel facts, devotional stories, finance
basics — any repeatable format can be set up with its own voice, style and structure.

#### AI video clips from a prompt

**Text to video.** Send a one-line prompt from your phone and receive AI video clips with sound in
a few minutes.

**Four options per request.** Every prompt returns four different eight-second clips, so you pick
the best take instead of trying again.

**Idea to video, on a schedule.** The factory can invent a viral-style idea, write a detailed
cinematic prompt for it, produce the clip, save it to your drive and log it in your content sheet —
on a timetable you choose.

#### Reel remix

**Send a reel link, get fresh versions.** Share the link of a reel that inspired you. The factory
downloads it, writes a detailed breakdown of what happens in it, and creates four new original clips
in the same spirit — for reels you own or have rights to use.

**Reel breakdown on its own.** Use just the breakdown to brief your team or plan your own shoot.

#### Publishing and control

**Upload to YouTube.** Finished Shorts upload to your channel with the title, description and tags the
factory wrote — as private, unlisted or public.

**Post to Instagram Reels.** Finished videos can go straight to your Instagram business account.

**Thumbnails included.** A matching thumbnail image is created for every video.

**Review from your phone.** Get each finished video in a chat on your phone and approve it before it
goes live.

**Content queue sheet.** Every topic, its status and the finished video are tracked in one simple
sheet you can read and edit.

**Your own storage.** All voice files, images and finished videos are kept in private storage, with
shareable links for each finished video.

### How it works

1. **We set up your formats.** Topics, story style, voice, language, caption look and music —
   agreed with you once.
2. **Pick your trigger.** Press start, send a prompt or a reel link from your phone, or let it run on
   a schedule.
3. **The factory writes.** It picks a fresh topic and writes the full script, scene by scene.
4. **It produces.** Voice-over, visuals, motion, captions and music are created and put together.
5. **You review.** The finished video comes to your phone and into your content sheet.
6. **It publishes.** Approved videos go to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

### FAQs

**How much does it cost?**
A one-time setup per format, then a monthly plan. AI usage — images, video clips, scripts — is billed
at actual cost or bundled into a fixed monthly video allowance.

**How long does setup take?**
About a week for a new format: we agree the style, test several videos with you and tune the voice,
captions and pacing.

**Do I need to show my face or record my voice?**
No. Everything — story, voice, visuals and captions — is created for you.

**Can it make videos in Hindi and other Indian languages?**
Yes. Hindi voice with Hinglish captions is our default for Indian audiences. Voices are also
available for Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu and Indian English.

**Will YouTube allow AI videos? Can the channel be monetised?**
YouTube allows AI-assisted videos. What it rewards is original, varied content, which is why every
video has a fresh topic and its own script. We also help you set the right disclosures, such as
"made for kids" for children's stories.

**Who owns the videos?**
You do. The scripts, voice-overs and finished videos are yours to publish anywhere.

**How many videos can it make?**
As many as your plan allows — one a day is a typical start. Each video is made one after another so
quality stays consistent.

**Can I check a video before it is posted?**
Yes. Finished videos come to your phone first, and you decide what goes live.

**Can my team use it from mobile?**
Yes. Prompts, reel links and approvals all work from a chat app on the phone.

**Is it safe to remix other people's reels?**
Use Reel remix for your own reels, reels you have permission to use, or as a breakdown to plan your
own original shoot. The new clips are freshly generated, not copies.

**Can it use my own music or voice style?**
Yes. We can set up your own music library and choose a voice, speed and tone that fits your brand.
