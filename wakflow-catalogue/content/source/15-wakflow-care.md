### The problem

1. **"Everything looked fine — and we lost leads for weeks."** Automations often fail quietly.
   The dashboard shows green while enquiries never reach your CRM, and nobody knows until sales
   drop.
2. **"Our server went down and nobody knew why."** When a system stops, every hour of diagnosis
   is an hour of missed WhatsApp chats, bookings and payments.
3. **"We have backups… we think."** A backup that was never checked, is stored on the same
   machine, or silently failed three weeks ago is not a backup.

### Key benefits

**Every lead accounted for.** Each morning we check that every WhatsApp and website enquiry from

**Backed up every night, off-site.** Your databases, WhatsApp logins, settings and the complete

**Built from real incidents.** Every check exists because a real failure taught us to look for

### Features

#### Nightly backups

**Nightly database backups.** Every database behind your WhatsApp, inbox, CRM and automations is
copied every night and checked to be restorable before it is uploaded.

**WhatsApp login backup.** The saved WhatsApp sessions are backed up too, so numbers can be
brought back without scanning a QR code again.

**Settings and configuration backup.** Every setting, configuration file and deployment recipe
is included — the pieces that take longest to rebuild by hand.

**Full system backup.** Separately, the entire system is copied every night in verified pieces,
so everything can be rebuilt on a new server if ever needed.

**Off-site and verified.** Backups go to secure cloud storage, and every file is compared with the
uploaded copy before the backup is marked successful.

**30 days of history.** Thirty daily copies and twelve weekly copies of your data, plus seven
nightly full-system copies.

**Low-impact timing.** Backups run in the early morning (India time), wait politely if the system
is busy, and never slow down your live chats.

**Restore help.** Written restore steps travel with every backup, and our team handles restores
for you.

#### Daily health check

**Every system checked every morning.** WhatsApp numbers, team inbox, CRM, automations, backups
and the server itself are inspected every day.

**Short daily summary.** A scannable message on Telegram or WhatsApp: a green tick, a warning or
a red flag per system, and a clear "action needed" line when something needs you.

**Full written log.** Every check's detail is kept in a dated log, so any issue can be traced back
to the day it started.

**Compares with yesterday.** Each check notes what is new, what is recurring and what has been
resolved.

#### Lead-loss monitoring

**Every enquiry traced end to end.** For each new WhatsApp contact and website enquiry, we check
it reached your lead sheet and then your CRM.

**Exact failure point named.** When a lead is missing, the check says where it broke — never
reached the automation, automation crashed, sheet refused the row, or CRM rejected it — so the
fix is fast.

**Overwritten records detected.** If a CRM record is ever overwritten by the wrong lead, it is
flagged with the original details so it can be restored.

**Bookings reconciled.** Paid bookings in your sheet are checked against the bookings in your CRM.

#### WhatsApp and inbox monitoring

**Number connection checks.** Every connected WhatsApp number is checked, and a number that has
logged out is flagged with how to reconnect it.

**Message sync checks.** We check that incoming WhatsApp messages — including messages from ad
clicks — are reaching your team inbox.

**Known-fault watch.** Faults we have fixed before are checked daily so they cannot quietly come
back.

**Background job health.** The inbox's background jobs are watched for failures and build-ups.

#### Automation monitoring

**Error alerts.** When an automation fails, our team gets an alert naming the automation, the
step and the error.

**Hidden-error inspection.** Every day we open a sample of "successful" runs and read their
results, catching failures that the system labelled as success.

**Wrong-destination guard.** We check that no old or duplicate automation has been switched back
on and is writing to the wrong place.

**Self-cleaning routines.** Automations that manage calls and recordings sweep up stuck items
every five minutes and report anything unusual.

#### CRM data protection

**Data-quality checks.** Daily checks that leads are filed under the right brand and source, and
that every new lead has its follow-up task.

**Record recovery.** Any CRM change can be traced to its before-and-after values, so damaged
records can be restored field by field.

#### Server health and security

**Load protection.** Server load is measured before anything heavy runs, and heavy jobs are
capped so they can never starve your live systems.

**Security checks.** Daily checks for exposed admin panels, default passwords, pending security
updates and unexpected changes.

**Disk and log hygiene.** Disk space, log sizes and stuck processes are checked daily.

**Website and routing checks.** Every public address is checked to still reach its application.

#### Maintenance and incident response

**Fixes with a paper trail.** Every change is made with a backup of what it replaces, recorded in
a running system record, and verified after.

**Careful recovery.** In an incident we stabilise first, protect data, and bring services back in
the right order.

**Updates and small changes.** Software updates, number re-connections and small changes to your
automations are part of the plan.

**Monthly report.** A monthly summary of uptime, backups, leads processed and issues fixed.

### How it works

1. **We map your systems.** Every number, inbox, database, automation and lead path is listed.
2. **Backups start the same night.** Databases, WhatsApp logins, settings and the full system go
   off-site, checked piece by piece.
3. **The daily check starts the next morning.** Every system is inspected and you get a short
   summary.
4. **Alerts come to our team first.** Failures, lost leads and disconnected numbers reach us, and
   we fix them — usually before you notice.
5. **Every incident becomes a check.** What went wrong once is watched for every day after.

### FAQs

**What exactly is backed up?**
Every database behind your WhatsApp, inbox, CRM and automations, your WhatsApp logins, all
settings and configuration — and, separately, a full copy of the whole system every night.

**Where are the backups stored?**
Off-site in secure cloud storage, not on the same machine. Each file is checked against the
uploaded copy before the backup counts as done.

**How far back can you restore?**
Thirty daily copies and twelve weekly copies of your data, and the last seven nights of the full
system.

**Will backups slow down my WhatsApp or inbox?**
No. They run in the early morning, use a small, capped share of the server, and wait if the
system is busy.

**How will I know if something is wrong?**
You get a short daily summary. Our team gets alerts the moment something fails, and we contact
you only when you need to act — for example, to re-link a WhatsApp number on your phone.

**Can you tell me if we are losing leads?**
Yes. Every morning we trace every new enquiry from the last day to your CRM and list any that did
not arrive, with the reason and the details needed to recover them.

**What if my WhatsApp number disconnects?**
We see it in the daily check and tell you exactly how to re-link it. Most reconnections take a
minute on your phone.

**Do you fix problems or only report them?**
Both. The daily check only observes, so it can never break anything. Fixes are made by our team,
with your approval for anything that changes data.

**Is my data safe with you?**
Access is limited to our team, every change is recorded, and we never delete data without your
explicit approval. Backups can be kept in a storage account you own.

**What does it cost?**
A monthly plan based on how many systems and numbers you run with us, billed yearly or monthly.
Cloud storage is included up to your plan's limit.

**Do I need Care if my systems are working fine?**
Our own systems were "working fine" while leads were quietly going missing. Care is how you find
out before your customers do.
