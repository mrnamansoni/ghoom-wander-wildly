### Replaces

Replaces the WordPress-plus-plugins website, the separate booking form tool, and the "please ask
the developer to change the price" routine.

### The problem

1. **"Every small change needs the developer."** A new price, a new batch date, a new photo. You
   message the web person, wait two days, and the old price stays online while customers see it.
2. **"The website looks nice but brings no business."** Enquiries go to an email nobody checks, or
   a form that silently fails. You find out weeks later that leads were lost.
3. **"Google does not show us."** The site has one page for everything, no proper titles, and
   pages that disappear whenever someone renames them, so years of ranking are thrown away.

### Key benefits

**You change it, it is live.** Press Save and the very next visitor sees the new price, date,
photo or line of text. There is nothing to publish, rebuild or wait for.

**Every part of the page is editable.** Products, prices, dates, locations, photos, videos,
headlines, reviews, FAQs, offers, team profiles, blog posts and whole page sections are all in
your dashboard. The design stays intact whatever you type.

**Enquiries and bookings that actually reach you.** Every form checks the phone number before it
accepts the lead, saves it in your dashboard and sends it to your CRM and sheet at once, tagged
with the page and the partner that brought it.

**Take payment online, safely.** Customers can pay a part-payment or advance by UPI or card. The
amount is always calculated by the website, never by the visitor's browser, and a booking counts
as paid only when the payment gateway confirms the exact amount.

**Built to rank on Google and to be read by AI assistants.** Every product, location, city and
article gets its own page with correct titles, structured data, FAQs and a live sitemap. Renamed
pages keep their old links working, so you never lose ranking by fixing a typo.

**Fast on Indian mobile networks.** Images are resized and compressed automatically for each phone.
Our flagship site scores 92 for mobile performance and a full 100 for accessibility, best practices
and SEO on Google's PageSpeed test.

### Features

#### Edit everything from your dashboard

**Products, listings and packages.** Each one has its own page: name, summary, full description,
day-by-day plan, what is included and excluded, paid add-ons, tips, a downloadable PDF, and a hero
photo or video. Keep it as a draft while you prepare it, then switch it live with one click.

**Prices by location and option.** Set a different price for each city or branch, and for each
option (for example twin, triple or quad sharing). Visitors see the price for their own city.

**Dates, batches and slots.** Add upcoming dates for each product and choose which cities each date
leaves from. Past dates drop off the site on their own.

**Locations and branches.** Add a city once and it gets its own landing page, its own prices and
its own list of upcoming dates.

**Every headline and line of text.** Around a hundred pieces of text across the homepage and the
main pages are editable, grouped by where they appear. Blank a field and it returns to the
original wording, so you can experiment without fear.

**Switch page sections on and off.** Hide or show whole bands of a page from one switchboard. The
page reflows neatly on save.

**Photos and videos, named by where they appear.** Every image spot on the site has a name, such
as "homepage hero" or "gallery". Replace one, add more to a gallery, or swap a photo everywhere it
is used in a single step. Upload from your phone or laptop, or paste a Google Drive link.

**Reviews, FAQs and team profiles.** Add customer reviews with rating and source, maintain your
FAQs, and show your team members with photo and a line about each.

**Partner and influencer pages.** Give a partner, creator or agent their own page with photo, bio,
their own dates and prices, and perks for people who book through them. Every lead from their page
is credited to them.

**Group and institution enquiries.** A dedicated page with a quote form for schools, colleges and
companies, with past group trips, photos and testimonials that you manage.

**Coupons and offers.** Create discount codes with an expiry date, minimum booking value, maximum
discount and a total-use limit. Pause a code at any time.

**Announcements and pop-ups.** A top announcement bar and a timed pop-up that asks visitors for
their number, both with your own text, image and timing.

**Blog and guides.** Write articles in the dashboard with a cover photo, a short answer at the top,
a questions-and-answers block and the one search phrase each article should rank for. Articles can
show live prices and next dates, so the numbers inside them are never out of date.

**Business details in one place.** WhatsApp number, social links, support hours, legal name,
address, grievance officer, bank details for invoices, analytics and ad-pixel IDs. Change them once
and they update across every page, policy and invoice.

#### Changes go live instantly

**No rebuild, no waiting.** Your content is read fresh on every visit, so Save means live. There is
no "publish" queue and no second save needed.

**Draft and live switches.** Prepare a new product or article in private and publish it when ready.
Switch online booking off for a single product while keeping its page and enquiry form.

**Rename safely.** Change a page address and the old address forwards permanently to the new one,
so Google, old WhatsApp forwards and Instagram bio links keep working.

**Protected against half-saved files.** Every save is written in one clean step, so a visitor can
never load a half-updated page.

#### Enquiries, bookings and payments

**Smart booking bar.** Visitors choose their city, date, number of people and sharing option, and
see the correct price before they commit.

**Hold-my-seat and enquiry forms.** Short forms on every product page, in the navigation and in a
timed pop-up. The phone number is checked for a valid Indian mobile before the lead is accepted.

**One tap to WhatsApp.** A visitor who prefers to chat is sent to your WhatsApp with a ready-made
message naming the product, city and date they were looking at.

**Online part-payment.** Take a seat hold, token or advance online, with GST added where it
applies. The rest is collected by your team later. The percentages are set in your dashboard.

**Payment you can trust.** The website calculates every amount itself, re-checks coupons at the
moment of payment, and marks a booking as paid only when the payment gateway confirms both the
status and the exact amount.

**Downloadable invoice.** After paying, the customer downloads a branded PDF invoice with a clear
payment summary. You can download any paid order's invoice from the dashboard too.

**All leads and payments in your dashboard.** See every enquiry with the page it came from, the
partner who sent it and the number of travellers or items. See every online payment with the
amount collected and the balance still due.

**Straight to your CRM and sheet.** Every enquiry and every paid booking is sent instantly to your
CRM and Google Sheet. A "send test" button and a list of recent deliveries show you it is working.

#### Found on Google and by AI assistants

**A page for everything people search for.** Products, destinations or categories, cities,
partners and articles each get their own page with its own title and description.

**Structured data built in.** Your business, products with prices, FAQs, breadcrumbs, articles and
people are described in the format Google uses for rich results. Star ratings are added
automatically once a product has enough real reviews.

**Automatic sitemap.** The sitemap lists only pages that are live right now and updates the moment
you publish or hide something.

**Old links never break.** Renamed and retired pages forward permanently to their replacement.

**Ready for AI search.** A plain-language summary of your whole business for AI assistants, and a
clean text version of each page that assistants can read and quote, while still asking them not to
train on your content.

**A site index on every page.** Every live product, city, destination and partner page is one
click away from anywhere on the site, which helps Google find new pages quickly.

#### Fast, beautiful and accessible

**Images sized for each phone.** Photos are compressed and served in modern formats at the size
each screen needs. On our flagship site a single photo went from 853 KB to 71 KB.

**Scroll-driven design that stays fast.** Rich animation and 3D scenes load after the page is
visible and switch off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

**Accessible to everyone.** Keyboard navigation, readable contrast, large touch targets and proper
labels for screen readers.

**Live weather on product pages.** Visitors see current conditions at the destination.

**Analytics that do not slow the page.** Your Google Analytics and Meta pixel load on the visitor's
first scroll or tap, so they do not hold up the page.

#### Safe and looked after

**Protected dashboard.** Password login with a strong hash, a signed session that expires, and an
automatic lockout after repeated wrong attempts.

**Payment keys stay private.** Gateway keys are entered in the dashboard and are never sent to a
visitor's browser.

**Nightly backups and daily checks.** Your site and its data are backed up every night, and a
daily check confirms your website enquiries are arriving.

**Hosting included.** We host it on your own domain, keep it secure and keep it fast.

### How it works

1. **We map your business.** On a short call we list what you sell, how you price it, your dates or
   slots, your locations and how you want enquiries handled.
2. **We design and build your site and dashboard.** Pages, design and dashboard fields are shaped
   around your products, not a generic template.
3. **We load your content.** Send us your existing price list or spreadsheet, photos and text; we
   import it so your site starts full.
4. **We connect the pipes.** Enquiries to your WhatsApp, CRM and sheet; online payment through your
   own gateway account; analytics and Google Search Console.
5. **You go live and take over.** We hand over the dashboard with a walkthrough. From then on you
   change prices, dates, photos and pages yourself.
6. **We keep it healthy.** Hosting, backups, security and daily checks stay with us.

### FAQs

**How is it priced?**
There is a one-time setup fee for design, build and loading your content, and a monthly fee for
hosting, backups, security and support. Payment gateway charges are paid by you directly to your
gateway.

**How long does it take to go live?**
It depends on how many products and pages you have. We agree the timeline on the first call, after
we have seen your price list and photos.

**Can I really change things myself?**
Yes. Prices, dates, photos, text, offers, reviews, FAQs, blog posts and whole page sections are all
edited from your dashboard, and the change is live as soon as you save.

**What if I make a mistake?**
Any text field can be reset to its original wording in one click. Renaming a page keeps the old link
working. And your data is backed up every night.

**Can my staff use it on mobile?**
The dashboard opens in any web browser, on a laptop or a phone. Each person needs the dashboard
login from you.

**Can the website be in Hindi or Hinglish?**
Yes. Every piece of text is yours to write, in English, Hindi or Hinglish, and we can set up the
pages in the language your customers use.

**Will it show up on Google?**
It is built for it: a separate page for each product, city and article, correct titles and
descriptions, structured data, an automatic sitemap and fast loading. Ranking still takes time and
good content, and we help you plan the pages people actually search for.

**Can customers pay online?**
Yes. You can take a token, seat hold or full amount by UPI or card through your own payment gateway
account. The money goes straight to your account.

**Where do the enquiries go?**
Into your dashboard, and at the same moment into your CRM and Google Sheet. You can also route them
to WhatsApp so your team or AI agent replies within seconds.

**Is my data safe? Who owns it?**
Your content and your leads belong to you. The dashboard is password-protected with a lockout
against guessing, payment keys are never exposed to visitors, and everything is backed up nightly.
You can ask us for a full copy of your content at any time.

**I already have a website. Can you move it?**
Yes. We move your content across and forward your old page addresses to the new ones, so you keep
the Google ranking you have already earned.

**Do I need to buy hosting separately?**
No. Hosting is included. You only need your domain name, and we can help you buy one.
